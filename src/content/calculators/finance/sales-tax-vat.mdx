## How to use this calculator

Enter a **net price** and **tax rate** to add tax. To **remove** tax from a price that already includes it, press × on the net price and type the gross price — the net price and tax are calculated.

## Formulas

<p class="formula">Gross = Net × (1 + rate)   Tax = Gross − Net<br/>Net = Gross ÷ (1 + rate)</p>

## Worked examples

**Adding 18%:** net 100 → tax 18 → gross **118**.

**Removing 20% VAT:** gross 120 → net = 120 ÷ 1.2 = **100**, VAT = **20**.

## A common mistake

To remove 20% VAT from 120, many people take 20% off: 120 × 0.8 = 96. That is wrong — the tax was 20% of the **net** price, not of the gross. The correct net is 100.
