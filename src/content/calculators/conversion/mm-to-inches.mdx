## How to use this converter

Type a value in **millimetres** or **inches** and the others update instantly. Useful for drawings, drill bits, fasteners and machining tolerances.

## Formula

<p class="formula">inches = mm ÷ 25.4   mm = inches × 25.4</p>

**Example:** 25 mm = 25 ÷ 25.4 = **0.984 in**. A 1/2″ bolt = 0.5 × 25.4 = **12.7 mm**.

## Thousandths of an inch (thou / mil)

Machinists often work in thousandths: 0.001″ = 0.0254 mm = 25.4 µm. A human hair is about 0.003″ (70–80 µm).

## Pipe sizes are nominal

A ½″ pipe does not measure 12.7 mm outside — nominal pipe sizes are trade names. A ½″ NPS steel pipe has an outside diameter of 21.3 mm. Always check the pipe standard.
