## How to use this calculator

Enter your **goal**, **current savings**, **interest rate** and **time frame**. The monthly amount you need to save appears. Change any field to explore — for example, how much sooner you would get there by saving a little more.

## The formula

<p class="formula">Goal = P₀(1 + i)<sup>n</sup> + M × ((1 + i)<sup>n</sup> − 1) ÷ i</p>

- **P₀** — current savings, growing with interest
- **M** — monthly deposit (end of each month)
- **i** = annual rate ÷ 12, **n** = months

## Worked example

Goal 50,000 in 5 years, 5,000 saved already, 7% interest:

- Current savings grow to about 7,088
- The remaining 42,912 must come from deposits
- Monthly saving needed = **599.39**

## Common goals

| Goal | Typical target |
|---|---|
| Emergency fund | 3–6 months of expenses |
| Home down payment | 10–20% of the price |
| Car | Price minus trade-in |
| Wedding or travel | Your estimated budget |

## Starting early vs saving more

To reach 50,000 with nothing saved yet, at 7% a year:

| Time to goal | Monthly saving needed |
|---|---|
| 2 years | 1,946.96 |
| 3 years | 1,252.19 |
| 5 years | 698.39 |
| 8 years | 390.02 |
| 10 years | 288.88 |

Doubling the time more than halves the monthly amount, because interest does more of the work.

## Where to keep goal savings

| Time to goal | Suitable options |
|---|---|
| Under 1 year | Savings account, liquid fund, short FD |
| 1–3 years | FDs, RDs, short-term debt funds |
| 3–5 years | Mix of debt and hybrid funds |
| Over 5 years | Mostly diversified equity funds |

The closer the goal, the less risk you can take, because there is no time to recover from a market fall.

## The 50/30/20 budget

A simple way to find money for goals: spend about 50% of take-home pay on needs, 30% on wants and put **20% into savings** and debt repayment. Automate the transfer on payday so you save before you spend.

## Emergency fund first

Before saving for a holiday or a car, build an emergency fund of 3–6 months of expenses. Use this calculator with that amount as your first goal.

## Tips

- Automate the transfer on payday so saving happens first.
- Increase the monthly amount whenever your income rises.
- For goals more than 5 years away, consider an [SIP](/finance/sip/) in diversified funds.
