    Scale and convert recipes

    The recipe scaler multiplies every ingredient for more or fewer servings. When a recipe uses cups but you weigh in grams, use grams to cups, which uses the real density of each ingredient: a cup of flour weighs about 120 g, but a cup of sugar about 200 g. The oven temperature converter switches between °C, °F and gas marks, and the cake pan converter adjusts a recipe for a different tin.

    Ratios that give better results

    Bakers write bread recipes as baker's percentages, with flour as 100%, which makes any recipe easy to scale. The rice to water calculator gives the ratio for basmati, brown or sushi rice, and the coffee ratio calculator sets grams of coffee per ml of water for pour-over, French press or espresso. The brine calculator gives salt per litre for pickling and wet brines.

    Planning food for a group

    For parties, estimate meat per person, drinks and pizza so you neither run short nor waste food. Restaurant owners can set menu prices with the food cost calculator; many aim for a food cost of 28% to 35% of the menu price.

    Frequently asked questions

    Why does a cup of flour weigh less than a cup of sugar?

    Flour is lighter and full of air. A cup of plain flour is about 120 g, while a cup of granulated sugar is about 200 g. That is why weighing gives more consistent results.

    Are cups the same in every country?

    No. A US cup is 236.6 ml, while the metric cup used in Australia and many Indian recipes is 250 ml, about 6% more. For small batches the difference rarely matters; for baking, weighing in grams is safest.

    How much rice should I cook per person?

    About 75 to 90 g of uncooked rice per adult as a side, or 100 g or more as a main dish.
