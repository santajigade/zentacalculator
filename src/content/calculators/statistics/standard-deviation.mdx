## How to use the standard deviation calculator

Paste or type your numbers, separated by commas, spaces or new lines — you can paste a column from Excel. All results update instantly.

## Standard deviation formulas

<p class="formula">s = √( Σ(x − x̄)² ÷ (n − 1) )   σ = √( Σ(x − μ)² ÷ n )</p>

## Worked example

Data: 4, 8, 6, 5, 3, 7, 9, 5 (n = 8)

- Mean = 47 ÷ 8 = **5.875**
- Sum of squared deviations = 28.875
- Sample variance = 4.1250 → **s = 2.0310**
- Population variance = 3.6094 → **σ = 1.8998**

## Interpreting standard deviation

For roughly bell-shaped data, about 68% of values fall within 1 SD of the mean and 95% within 2 SD. See the [empirical rule calculator](/statistics/empirical-rule/).

## Step-by-step example

Data: 4, 8, 6, 5, 7.

| x | x − mean (6) | (x − mean)² |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | −2 | 4 |
| 8 | 2 | 4 |
| 6 | 0 | 0 |
| 5 | −1 | 1 |
| 7 | 1 | 1 |
| | **Sum** | **10** |

- Sample variance = 10 ÷ (5 − 1) = 2.5, sample SD = **1.581**
- Population variance = 10 ÷ 5 = 2, population SD = **1.414**

## Sample or population?

- Use **sample** SD (n − 1) when your data are a sample from a larger group — the usual case.
- Use **population** SD (n) when you have every member of the group.

Dividing by n − 1 (Bessel's correction) makes the sample variance an unbiased estimate of the population variance.

## Standard deviation vs standard error

The SD describes the spread of individual values. The standard error (SD ÷ √n) describes how precisely the mean is estimated. See the [standard error calculator](/statistics/standard-error/).

## Units

The SD has the same units as the data; the variance has squared units. That is why SD is easier to interpret: “heights vary by about 7 cm” rather than “49 cm²”.

## In Excel

Sample: `=STDEV.S(range)` · Population: `=STDEV.P(range)`

## How much data falls within 1, 2 and 3 standard deviations?

| Range | Share of data (normal distribution) | Outside the range |
|---|---|---|
| Mean ± 1 SD | 68.27% | 1 in 3 |
| Mean ± 2 SD | 95.45% | 1 in 22 |
| Mean ± 3 SD | 99.73% | 1 in 370 |
| Mean ± 4 SD | 99.994% | 1 in 15,787 |

## Standard deviation functions in software

| Tool | Sample SD | Population SD |
|---|---|---|
| Excel / Google Sheets | STDEV.S(range) | STDEV.P(range) |
| Python (statistics) | stdev(data) | pstdev(data) |
| NumPy | np.std(x, ddof=1) | np.std(x) |
| R | sd(x) | sqrt(mean((x − mean(x))²)) |
| Casio calculator | sx or σn−1 | σx or σn |
