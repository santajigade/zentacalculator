## How to use this calculator

Enter the **weight you want to lose** and your planned **daily calorie deficit**. The estimated time appears in weeks (switch to days or months). Find your maintenance calories first with the [BMR & daily calorie calculator](/health/bmr/), then subtract your deficit.

This calculator is limited to deficits of up to 1,000 kcal per day.

## The formula

<p class="formula">Time (days) = weight to lose (kg) × 7,700 ÷ daily deficit</p>

## Worked example

Losing 5 kg with a 500 kcal daily deficit:

5 × 7,700 ÷ 500 = 77 days ≈ **11 weeks**

## Rule-of-thumb rates

| Daily deficit | Approx. loss per week |
|---|---|
| 250 kcal | 0.23 kg (0.5 lb) |
| 500 kcal | 0.45 kg (1 lb) |
| 750 kcal | 0.68 kg (1.5 lb) |
| 1,000 kcal | 0.91 kg (2 lb) |

## Important

- Very low-calorie diets should only be followed under medical supervision.
- Combine a moderate deficit with enough protein and regular activity to protect muscle.
- If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or have a history of eating difficulties, speak to a doctor or registered dietitian before changing your diet.
