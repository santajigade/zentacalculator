## How to use this calculator

Enter the coefficients **a**, **b** and **c** of ax² + bx + c = 0. The discriminant and both roots appear instantly. Click **Show steps** to see the formula with your numbers.

If the discriminant is negative, the roots are complex and the calculator shows a warning — there is no real x that solves the equation.

## The quadratic formula

<p class="formula">x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) ÷ 2a</p>

## Worked example

2x² − 7x + 3 = 0, so a = 2, b = −7, c = 3.

- Discriminant = (−7)² − 4 × 2 × 3 = 49 − 24 = **25**
- x₁ = (7 + 5) ÷ 4 = **3**
- x₂ = (7 − 5) ÷ 4 = **0.5**

Check: 2(3)² − 7(3) + 3 = 18 − 21 + 3 = 0 ✓

## Discriminant cases

| Discriminant | Roots |
|---|---|
| Positive | Two different real roots |
| Zero | One repeated real root |
| Negative | Two complex roots (no real solution) |

## Sum and product of roots

For any quadratic, x₁ + x₂ = −b ÷ a and x₁ × x₂ = c ÷ a. In the example: 3 + 0.5 = 3.5 = 7/2 and 3 × 0.5 = 1.5 = 3/2.
