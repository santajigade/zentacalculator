## How to use this calculator

Enter your **current annual expenses**, expected **inflation**, **years until retirement** and a **withdrawal rate**. The calculator projects your expenses at retirement and the corpus needed to fund them.

## Formulas

<p class="formula">Expenses at retirement = E × (1 + inflation)<sup>t</sup><br/>Corpus = Expenses at retirement ÷ withdrawal rate</p>

## Worked example

Annual expenses of 600,000 today, 6% inflation, retiring in 25 years, 4% withdrawal rate:

- Expenses at retirement = 600,000 × 1.06²⁵ = **2,575,122 per year**
- Corpus needed = 2,575,122 ÷ 0.04 = **64,378,061**

That large figure shows how strongly inflation compounds over decades.

## Withdrawal rate and corpus multiple

| Withdrawal rate | Corpus = expenses × |
|---|---|
| 5% | 20 |
| 4% | 25 |
| 3.5% | 28.6 |
| 3% | 33.3 |

## Important assumptions

- Investments keep growing during retirement, roughly matching inflation plus the withdrawal rate.
- Healthcare costs often rise faster than general inflation.
- Pensions or rental income reduce the corpus needed — subtract them from expenses.

This is a planning estimate, not financial advice. Consider speaking to a qualified financial planner.
