## How to use this calculator

Enter your **daily calories** and the **protein** and **carbohydrate** percentages. Fat is whatever remains to make 100%. The grams of each macronutrient are calculated.

## Formulas

<p class="formula">Protein g = calories × protein % ÷ 4<br/>Carbs g = calories × carb % ÷ 4<br/>Fat g = calories × fat % ÷ 9</p>

## Worked example

2,200 kcal with 30% protein, 40% carbohydrate, 30% fat:

- Protein = 2,200 × 0.30 ÷ 4 = **165 g**
- Carbohydrate = 2,200 × 0.40 ÷ 4 = **220 g**
- Fat = 2,200 × 0.30 ÷ 9 = **73 g**

## Common splits

| Goal | Protein | Carbs | Fat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Balanced | 20% | 50% | 30% |
| Fat loss | 30–35% | 35–40% | 25–30% |
| Muscle gain | 25–30% | 45–50% | 20–25% |
| Lower carb | 30% | 20–25% | 45–50% |

Quality matters as much as quantity: choose whole grains, lean proteins, vegetables and healthy fats. People with diabetes or other conditions should plan diets with a doctor or dietitian.
