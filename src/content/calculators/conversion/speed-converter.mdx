## How to use this converter

Type a speed in **any** unit and the others update instantly.

## Formula

<p class="formula">mph = km/h × 0.621371   m/s = km/h ÷ 3.6   1 knot = 1.852 km/h</p>

**Example:** 100 km/h = **62.1 mph** = **27.8 m/s** = **54.0 knots**.

## Quick conversions

- **km/h → m/s:** divide by 3.6 (36 km/h = 10 m/s)
- **km/h → mph:** multiply by 0.6 for an estimate (100 km/h ≈ 60 mph)
- **Knots** are used at sea and in aviation; 1 knot = 1 nautical mile per hour.
