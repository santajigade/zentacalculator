## How to use this calculator

Enter the **bill**, the **tip percentage** and the **number of people**. The tip, total and amount per person are calculated.

## Example

A bill of 85 with a 15% tip, split 4 ways:

- Tip = **12.75**
- Total = **97.75**
- Each person pays **24.44**
