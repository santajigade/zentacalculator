## How to use this calculator

Enter a **future amount**, the **discount rate** and the **number of years** to get its present value. To go the other way, press × on Future amount and type today's value.

## The formula

<p class="formula">PV = FV ÷ (1 + r)<sup>t</sup></p>

## Worked example

You will receive 10,000 in 10 years. With an 8% discount rate:

PV = 10,000 ÷ 1.08¹⁰ = **4,631.93**

So 10,000 in 10 years is worth about the same as 4,632 today — because 4,632 invested at 8% would grow to 10,000.

## Why money now is worth more

- It can be invested to earn a return.
- Inflation reduces what future money buys.
- Future payments carry a risk of not arriving.

## Uses

- Comparing a lump sum today with payments later (lottery, settlements, pensions).
- Valuing bonds and business projects (net present value).
- Deciding whether to pay a bill early for a discount.
