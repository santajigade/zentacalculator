## How to use this calculator

Enter **one angle** and **one side**, or **two sides**. All remaining sides and angles are calculated. Angles can be entered in degrees or radians.

## Formulas (SOH CAH TOA)

<p class="formula">sin A = a ÷ c   cos A = b ÷ c   tan A = a ÷ b<br/>A + B = 90°</p>

## Worked example

A hypotenuse of 10 m at angle A = 30°:

- a = 10 × sin 30° = **5 m**
- b = 10 × cos 30° = **8.66 m**
- B = 90° − 30° = **60°**

**Ladder check:** a 6 m ladder at 75° (the recommended safe angle) reaches 6 × sin 75° = **5.80 m** high with its foot 6 × cos 75° = **1.55 m** from the wall.
