## How to use the rectangle calculator

Enter the **length** and **width** to get the area, perimeter and diagonal. Or enter any two values — for example area and length — to find the rest. Area units (m², ft², acres…) are converted automatically.

## Rectangle formulas

<p class="formula">A = l × w   P = 2(l + w)   d = √(l² + w²)</p>

## Worked example

A room 8 m long and 5 m wide:

- Area = 8 × 5 = **40 m²** (430.6 ft²)
- Perimeter = 2 × (8 + 5) = **26 m** (skirting board needed)
- Diagonal = √(64 + 25) = **9.43 m**

## Practical uses

- **Flooring and tiles:** buy area + 5–10% for cuts and waste.
- **Paint:** wall area = perimeter × ceiling height, minus doors and windows.
- **Fencing:** perimeter of a rectangular plot.

## Same perimeter, different area

With 40 m of fencing:

| Shape | Area enclosed |
|---|---|
| 15 × 5 | 75 m² |
| 12 × 8 | 96 m² |
| 10 × 10 (square) | 100 m² |

A square gives the most area for a given perimeter.

## Area of an L-shaped room

Split the shape into two rectangles, find each area and add them. A room 6 × 4 m with a 2 × 3 m alcove has an area of 24 + 6 = **30 m²**.

## Rooms with sloped or irregular walls

For a room that is not quite rectangular, measure the length at both ends and the width at both ends, average each pair, then multiply. For larger irregular areas, break them into rectangles and triangles. See the [triangle area calculator](/math/triangle-area/).
