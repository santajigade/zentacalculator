## How to use this calculator

Choose **sex**, then enter **age**, **body weight** and **serum creatinine** in mg/dL. The estimated creatinine clearance appears in mL/min.

## The formula (Cockcroft–Gault, 1976)

<p class="formula">CrCl = (140 − age) × weight (kg) ÷ (72 × creatinine (mg/dL))   × 0.85 for women</p>

## Worked example

A 60-year-old man weighing 70 kg with creatinine 1.0 mg/dL:

CrCl = 80 × 70 ÷ 72 = **77.8 mL/min**

The same values for a woman give 77.8 × 0.85 = **66.1 mL/min**.

## Interpretation (approximate)

| CrCl (mL/min) | Kidney function |
|---|---|
| 90 or more | Normal |
| 60–89 | Mildly reduced |
| 30–59 | Moderately reduced |
| 15–29 | Severely reduced |
| below 15 | Kidney failure |

## Important

The formula is less accurate in people with very high or low body weight, older adults, amputees and during rapidly changing kidney function. It is a clinical estimation tool — diagnosis and dosing decisions belong to qualified healthcare professionals.
