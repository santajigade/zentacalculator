## How to use this calculator

Enter the average **cigarettes per day** and the **number of years** you smoked at that level. If your habit changed over time, calculate each period separately and add the results.

## The formula

<p class="formula">Pack-years = (cigarettes per day ÷ 20) × years smoked</p>

## Worked examples

- 20 cigarettes a day for 20 years = **20 pack-years**
- 30 a day for 20 years = **30 pack-years**
- 10 a day for 15 years, then 20 a day for 10 years = 7.5 + 10 = **17.5 pack-years**

## It's never too late to quit

Risk starts to fall soon after stopping and keeps falling for years. Free support is available in most countries through national quit lines and health services — ask your doctor or pharmacist.
