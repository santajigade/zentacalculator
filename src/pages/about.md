Zenta Calculator is a growing library of free, interactive calculators for everyday life, finance, health, science, engineering and more.

## What makes our calculators different

- **Solve for any value.** Fill in what you know — any fields — and the rest is calculated.
- **See the working.** Every result can show its step-by-step solution.
- **Explore with charts.** Plot how one value changes with another.
- **Your units, your currency.** Units switch instantly, and the site adapts to your region.
- **Private by design.** Calculations run in your browser. No sign-up, no account.

## Our approach to accuracy

Each calculator page explains its formula, assumptions and limits, with worked examples you can check by hand. We update pages when standards or rules change and show the date of the last update on every page.
