    Weight and body composition

    The BMI calculator is a quick screening tool, but it cannot tell muscle from fat. For a fuller picture, add the waist to height ratio, which is a better guide to health risk from belly fat, and estimate body fat with the US Navy tape method. The ideal weight calculator shows the range given by the common formulas, and lean body mass is useful for setting protein targets.

    Calories, food and fitness

    Your BMR is the energy you burn at rest. Multiply it by your activity level to get daily calories, then split them with the macro calculator. The weight loss time calculator shows how long a goal takes at a safe deficit. For training, set heart rate zones, estimate VO₂ max and convert steps to distance.

    Clinical calculators for professionals

    Doctors, nurses and pharmacists use the eGFR (CKD-EPI 2021) and creatinine clearance (Cockcroft-Gault) calculators for kidney function and drug dosing, QTc for ECG intervals, the IV drip rate for infusions and LDL cholesterol from a lipid panel. These tools follow the published formulas, but results must always be read by a qualified professional together with the full clinical picture.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are these calculators medical advice?

    No. They give estimates for general information. Speak to a doctor about your health.

    Is BMI accurate for athletes?

    Not very. Muscular people can have a BMI in the overweight range with low body fat. Use body fat percentage or waist to height ratio alongside BMI.

    Which BMI cut-offs are used for Asian adults?

    Many Asian health bodies, including in India, use lower cut-offs: overweight from 23 and obesity from 25 (or 27.5 in some guidelines), because health risks rise at lower BMI.
