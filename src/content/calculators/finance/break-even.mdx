## How to use this calculator

Enter your **fixed costs** (rent, salaries, insurance for the period), **selling price** and **variable cost per unit**. You get the units and revenue needed to break even. To set a price for a sales target, press × on the price and type the units you expect to sell.

## Formulas

<p class="formula">Break-even units = Fixed costs ÷ (Price − Variable cost)<br/>Break-even revenue = units × price</p>

## Worked example

Fixed costs of 50,000 a month, product sells for 40 and costs 15 to make:

- Contribution margin = 40 − 15 = 25 per unit
- Break-even = 50,000 ÷ 25 = **2,000 units**
- Revenue at break-even = 2,000 × 40 = **80,000**

Every unit sold beyond 2,000 adds 25 of profit.

## Fixed vs variable costs

| Fixed (don't change with sales) | Variable (per unit sold) |
|---|---|
| Rent | Raw materials |
| Salaries | Packaging |
| Insurance | Shipping |
| Software subscriptions | Sales commission |

## Margin of safety

If you expect to sell 2,600 units, your margin of safety is 600 units (23%) — how far sales could fall before you make a loss.
