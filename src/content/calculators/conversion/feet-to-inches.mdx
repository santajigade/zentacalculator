## How to use the feet to inches converter

Type a value in **Feet** and the other units update instantly. Every field is live, so you can just as easily start from inches or any other unit and convert back.

## Feet to inches formula

<p class="formula">inches = feet × 12</p>

1 ft = 12 in, and 1 in = 0.0833333 ft.

## Decimal feet

Surveying and engineering drawings often use decimal feet. 5.75 ft = 5 ft 9 in, because 0.75 × 12 = 9. To convert inches to decimal feet, divide by 12: 8 in = 0.667 ft.

## Lumber lengths

Boards are sold in standard lengths: 8 ft (96 in), 10 ft (120 in), 12 ft (144 in) and 16 ft (192 in). See the [board feet calculator](/construction/board-feet/).
