## How to use this calculator

Enter your **start time**, **end time** and **unpaid break** in minutes. Add your **hourly rate** to see the pay for the shift. Night shifts that pass midnight are handled automatically.

## Example

9:00 to 17:30 with a 30-minute break: 8.5 − 0.5 = **8 hours**. At 20 per hour, pay = **160**.

## Minutes to decimal hours

|---|---|
| 15 | 0.25 |
| 20 | 0.33 |
| 30 | 0.5 |
| 45 | 0.75 |

## Night shifts

Shifts that cross midnight are handled automatically: 22:00 to 06:00 is 8 hours, not −16. Enter the times in 24-hour format or with AM/PM.

## Rounding time

Some employers round clock-in and clock-out times to the nearest 5, 10 or 15 minutes. Rounding rules must usually be applied fairly in both directions. Keep your own record so you can check your pay.

## Overtime

Many employers pay overtime above 8 hours a day or 40 hours a week, often at 1.5× the normal rate. Check your contract and local labour law.
