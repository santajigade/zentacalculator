## How to use this calculator

Enter the two **parallel sides** and the **height** (the perpendicular distance between them). The area appears instantly. You can also solve for a missing side or height from a known area.

## The formula

<p class="formula">A = ½ (a + b) × h</p>

## Worked example

A trapezoid with parallel sides of 6 m and 10 m, 4 m apart:

A = ½ × (6 + 10) × 4 = **32 m²**

## Practical uses

- **Irregular plots of land** are often split into trapezoids and triangles.
- **Open channels and ditches** have trapezoidal cross-sections — multiply the area by flow velocity to estimate flow.
- **Roof sections** and **retaining walls** are frequently trapezoidal.

## Check with a rectangle

If both parallel sides are equal (a = b), the shape is a rectangle and the formula becomes a × h — the same as the [rectangle calculator](/math/rectangle/).
