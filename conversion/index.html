    Everyday and travel conversions

    Quick one-way converters cover the questions people search most: cm to inches, feet to metres, kg to lb, miles to km, °F to °C, gallons to litres and mph to km/h. Each shows the exact factor, a worked example and a table of common values, so you can convert in your head next time. Note that US and UK (imperial) gallons differ by about 20%, which matters for fuel economy and recipes.

    Indian land and number units

    Land in India is still measured in local units that vary by state. The gaj to sq ft, guntha to sq ft and cent to sq ft converters use the standard values, and the acres to hectares and area converter handle everything else. Always check the local value on your property documents, because a bigha or a gaj can differ between regions. The number to million converter switches between lakh and crore and the international million and billion system.

    Engineering units

    For plant and design work there are full converters for pressure (bar, psi, kPa, mmH₂O, inHg), flow rate, power, energy, torque and temperature, plus shortcuts like psi to bar and kW to hp. All factors follow the SI definitions, for example 1 psi = 6,894.757 Pa and 1 inch = 25.4 mm exactly.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are the conversion factors exact?

    Yes — they use exact SI definitions where they exist (for example 1 inch = 2.54 cm and 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg).

    Do you support Indian land units?

    Yes: gaj (square yard), guntha, cent, acre and hectare.

    Why does horsepower give two different answers?

    Mechanical horsepower (hp) is 745.7 W, while metric horsepower (PS) is 735.5 W. Motor nameplates in India and Europe usually use kW, and older equipment may use either hp.

    How big is a gaj?

    In most of North India 1 gaj (square yard) is 9 sq ft, or 0.836 m². Some local land records use slightly different values, so check your documents.
