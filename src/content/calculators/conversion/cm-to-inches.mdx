## How to use this converter

Type a value in **centimetres** or **inches** (or mm or feet) and the others update as you type.

## Formula

<p class="formula">inches = cm ÷ 2.54   cm = inches × 2.54</p>

**Example:** a 30 cm ruler is 30 ÷ 2.54 = **11.81 inches**. A 15.6-inch laptop screen is 15.6 × 2.54 = **39.6 cm** across the diagonal.

## Height in feet and inches

To convert a height in cm to feet and inches, divide by 2.54 to get inches, then divide by 12: 170 cm = 66.93 in = **5 ft 6.9 in**. See the [feet to metres converter](/conversion/feet-to-meters/) for a full height table.

## Definition

Since 1959 the inch has been defined as **exactly 2.54 cm**, so conversions between them are exact.

## Fractions of an inch

Imperial tools often show fractions: 1/16″ = 1.5875 mm, 1/8″ = 3.175 mm, 1/4″ = 6.35 mm, 1/2″ = 12.7 mm. Use the [fraction to decimal converter](/math/fraction-to-decimal/) to turn a fraction into a decimal inch first.

## Screen and TV sizes

Screen sizes are measured diagonally in inches: a 55″ TV is about 140 cm, and a 6.1″ phone screen is about 15.5 cm.
