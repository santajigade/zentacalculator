## How to use this calculator

Enter your **body weight** and how many **minutes you exercise** on a typical day. The estimate is shown in litres (or US gallons).

## The formula used

<p class="formula">Water (L) ≈ 0.033 × weight (kg) + 0.35 × (exercise minutes ÷ 30)</p>

## Worked example

A 70 kg adult who exercises 30 minutes a day:

- Base = 0.033 × 70 = 2.31 L
- Exercise = 0.35 L
- Total ≈ **2.66 L per day**

## Drink more when…

- it is hot or humid
- you are at high altitude
- you have a fever, vomiting or diarrhoea
- you are pregnant or breastfeeding (follow medical advice)

## A simple check

Pale yellow urine usually means you are well hydrated. Dark yellow urine and thirst are signs to drink more.

This is a general estimate. Individual needs vary.
