## How to use this calculator

Choose your **sex**, then enter your **height**, **neck** and **waist** measurements. Women also enter their **hip** measurement (men can ignore it). Measure in cm or inches.

## The formulas (measurements in cm)

<p class="formula">Men: %BF = 495 ÷ (1.0324 − 0.19077·log₁₀(waist − neck) + 0.15456·log₁₀(height)) − 450<br/>Women: %BF = 495 ÷ (1.29579 − 0.35004·log₁₀(waist + hip − neck) + 0.22100·log₁₀(height)) − 450</p>

## Worked examples

- **Man:** height 178 cm, neck 38 cm, waist 85 cm → about **16.4%**
- **Woman:** height 165 cm, neck 33 cm, waist 75 cm, hips 100 cm → about **29.4%**

## General body fat ranges

| Category | Men | Women |
|---|---|---|
| Essential fat | 2–5% | 10–13% |
| Athletes | 6–13% | 14–20% |
| Fitness | 14–17% | 21–24% |
| Average | 18–24% | 25–31% |
| High | 25%+ | 32%+ |

Source: American Council on Exercise guidelines.

## Tips for consistent results

Measure in the morning before eating, take each measurement three times and use the average. Track the trend over weeks rather than any single reading.

This is an estimate, not a medical assessment.
