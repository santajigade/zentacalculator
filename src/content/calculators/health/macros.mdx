## How to use the macro calculator

Enter your **daily calories** and the **protein** and **carbohydrate** percentages. Fat is whatever remains to make 100%. The grams of each macronutrient are calculated.

## Macro formulas

<p class="formula">Protein g = calories × protein % ÷ 4<br/>Carbs g = calories × carb % ÷ 4<br/>Fat g = calories × fat % ÷ 9</p>

## Worked example

2,200 kcal with 30% protein, 40% carbohydrate, 30% fat:

- Protein = 2,200 × 0.30 ÷ 4 = **165 g**
- Carbohydrate = 2,200 × 0.40 ÷ 4 = **220 g**
- Fat = 2,200 × 0.30 ÷ 9 = **73 g**

## Recommended ranges

The US National Academies give these acceptable ranges for adults:

| Macronutrient | Share of calories |
|---|---|
| Carbohydrate | 45–65% |
| Fat | 20–35% |
| Protein | 10–35% |

Higher-protein plans (25–35%) are popular for weight loss and muscle gain because protein is filling and helps keep muscle.

## Good sources of each macro

- **Protein**: eggs, dairy, fish, chicken, lentils, beans, tofu, paneer.
- **Carbohydrate**: whole grains, oats, rice, fruit, vegetables, legumes.
- **Fat**: nuts, seeds, olive oil, avocado, oily fish.

Choose mostly whole foods. The quality of each macro matters as much as the amount.

## Fibre and alcohol

- Aim for **25–38 g of fibre** a day from vegetables, fruit, whole grains and legumes.
- Alcohol has 7 kcal per gram. If you drink, count it in your daily calories.

## Common splits

| Goal | Protein | Carbs | Fat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Balanced | 20% | 50% | 30% |
| Fat loss | 30–35% | 35–40% | 25–30% |
| Muscle gain | 25–30% | 45–50% | 20–25% |
| Lower carb | 30% | 20–25% | 45–50% |

Quality matters as much as quantity: choose whole grains, lean proteins, vegetables and healthy fats. People with diabetes or other conditions should plan diets with a doctor or dietitian.
