    Frequently asked questions

    How do I convert rpm to g force?

    RCF = 1.118 × 10⁻⁵ × r × rpm², where r is the rotor radius in cm. For example, 10,000 rpm on a 10 cm rotor is about 11,180 × g.

    What is a good 260/280 ratio for DNA?

    About 1.8 for pure DNA and about 2.0 for pure RNA. Lower values suggest protein or phenol contamination.

    Are these calculators suitable for lab protocols?
