## How to use the length converter

Type a length into **any** field and every other unit updates instantly. Each field is linked, so you can start from feet, miles or millimetres just as easily as from metres.

## Length formula

<p class="formula">1 in = 2.54 cm (exact)   1 ft = 12 in = 0.3048 m   1 yd = 3 ft   1 mi = 1,760 yd = 1.609344 km</p>

All imperial lengths are defined from the inch, which has been exactly 2.54 cm since the 1959 international yard and pound agreement — so these conversions are exact, not approximations.

## Which unit to use

- **Metric (mm, cm, m, km)** — used for almost all science, engineering and everyday life worldwide.
- **Imperial / US customary (in, ft, yd, mi)** — everyday use in the United States; feet and inches are also common for heights and screen sizes elsewhere.

## Imperial relationships

- 12 inches = 1 foot
- 3 feet = 1 yard
- 1,760 yards = 1 mile = 5,280 feet

## Accuracy in practice

Builders typically measure to the nearest millimetre, machinists to 0.01 mm or less, and surveyors to a few millimetres over long distances. Round your converted values to match the precision of the original measurement.

## Quick mental shortcuts

- 1 metre ≈ 3 ft 3 in
- 30 cm ≈ 1 foot
- 1 km ≈ 0.62 miles; 8 km ≈ 5 miles
