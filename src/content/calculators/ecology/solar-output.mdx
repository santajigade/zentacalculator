## How to use the solar panel output calculator

Enter the **system size** in kWp, your location's **peak sun hours** and a **performance ratio**. Daily, monthly and yearly energy are estimated.

## Solar panel output formula

<p class="formula">kWh/day = kWp × peak sun hours × performance ratio</p>

## Example

A 3 kWp system with 5 peak sun hours at 75%:

- **11.3 kWh/day**
- **338 kWh/month**
- **4,106 kWh/year**

## Performance losses

The performance ratio (PR) accounts for:

- Inverter losses (2–4%)
- Temperature losses (hot panels lose 0.3–0.5% per °C above 25 °C)
- Dust and soiling
- Wiring losses and shading

A PR of 0.75–0.85 is typical for well-installed systems.

## Panel orientation

In the northern hemisphere, panels facing south at a tilt close to the latitude give the most yearly energy. East–west layouts produce less in total but spread generation across the day.
