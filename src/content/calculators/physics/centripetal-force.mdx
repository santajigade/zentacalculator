## How to use this calculator

Enter the **mass**, **speed** and **radius** to get the centripetal acceleration and force. Or find the maximum safe speed for a given radius and force.

## Formulas

<p class="formula">a = v² ÷ r   F = m v² ÷ r</p>

## Worked example

A 1,200 kg car takes a 50 m radius bend at 60 km/h (16.67 m/s):

- a = 16.67² ÷ 50 = **5.56 m/s²** (0.57 g)
- F = 1,200 × 5.56 = **6.67 kN**

## Grip limit

On a dry road, tyres can typically provide around 0.7–0.9 g of sideways acceleration; on wet roads much less. At 0.7 g, the maximum speed for a 50 m bend is about √(0.7 × 9.81 × 50) = 67 km/h.
