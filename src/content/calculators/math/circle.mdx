## How to use this calculator

Type **any one** value — radius, diameter, circumference or area — and the other three are calculated. Change the unit of any field independently; area units (cm², m², ft²…) convert automatically.

## The formulas

<p class="formula">d = 2r   C = πd = 2πr   A = πr²</p>

Working backwards:

- From circumference: r = C ÷ (2π)
- From area: r = √(A ÷ π)

## Worked example

A circular table has a diameter of 1.2 m.

- Radius = 1.2 ÷ 2 = 0.6 m
- Circumference = π × 1.2 ≈ **3.77 m** (the length of edge trim needed)
- Area = π × 0.6² ≈ **1.13 m²** (the tabletop surface)

## How area grows with size

Area depends on the **square** of the radius. Double the radius and the area becomes four times larger. That is why a 12-inch pizza has more than twice the area of an 8-inch pizza (113 in² vs 50 in²). The chart shows this curve.
