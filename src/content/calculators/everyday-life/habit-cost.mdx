## How to use the daily habit cost calculator

Enter the **cost** of the habit and how many **times per week** you do it. The monthly, yearly and ten-year totals are calculated.

## Example

A 4.50 coffee 5 times a week:

- **97.50 per month**
- **1,170 per year**
- **11,700 over 10 years**

## Putting it to work

Invested monthly at 8% a year instead, 97.50 a month grows to roughly 17,800 over 10 years. Try different amounts in the [SIP calculator](/finance/sip/).

## Cutting back without quitting

- **Reduce frequency**: going from 5 to 3 times a week cuts the cost by 40%.
- **Swap**: make coffee at home on most days and keep the café for Fridays.
- **Batch**: cook once and take lunch for several days.
- **Pause subscriptions** you rarely use.

## Spending you value

Not every habit needs cutting. If a daily coffee genuinely improves your day, keep it and cut something you value less. The point is to choose consciously.
