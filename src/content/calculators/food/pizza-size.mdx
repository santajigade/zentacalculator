## How to use the pizza size & value calculator

Enter the **diameter** and **price** of two pizzas. Their areas are calculated, plus how much more pizza B gives and how much cheaper it is per square inch.

## Example

12-inch for 12 vs 16-inch for 18:

- Areas: **113 in²** vs **201 in²** — B is **78%** bigger
- B is **15.6%** cheaper per square inch

## Why the big pizza is usually better value

Area grows with the square of the diameter. A 16-inch pizza has 78% more pizza than a 12-inch, but is rarely priced 78% higher.

## Crust matters

A larger pizza has proportionally less crust, so more of the area is topped. For crust lovers, smaller pizzas give more crust per person.
