## How to use this calculator

Enter **wins**, **draws**, **losses** and **goals for and against**. Points, points per game and goal difference are calculated.

## Example

18 wins, 8 draws, 6 losses, 55–30 goals:

- Points = **62**
- Points per game = **1.94**
- Goal difference = **+25**
