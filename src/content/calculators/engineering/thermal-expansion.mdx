## How to use this calculator

Choose the **material**, then enter the **length** and **temperature change**. The expansion is shown in mm or inches. A negative temperature change gives contraction.

## The formula

<p class="formula">ΔL = α × L × ΔT</p>

## Worked example

A 50 m carbon steel steam line heated from 20 °C to 80 °C (ΔT = 60 K):

ΔL = 12 × 10⁻⁶ × 50 × 60 = 0.036 m = **36 mm**

The same run in HDPE would grow about **600 mm** — plastic pipes need much more allowance.

## Allowing for expansion

Long hot lines need expansion loops, bellows or offsets, and guides and anchors placed to direct the movement. Restraining expansion completely creates very large forces that can damage supports, flanges and connected equipment.
