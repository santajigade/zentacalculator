## How to use this calculator

Enter the **weight you want to lose** and your planned **daily calorie deficit**. The estimated time appears in weeks (switch to days or months). Find your maintenance calories first with the [BMR & daily calorie calculator](/health/bmr/), then subtract your deficit.

This calculator is limited to deficits of up to 1,000 kcal per day.

## The formula

<p class="formula">Time (days) = weight to lose (kg) × 7,700 ÷ daily deficit</p>

## Worked example

Losing 5 kg with a 500 kcal daily deficit:

5 × 7,700 ÷ 500 = 77 days ≈ **11 weeks**

## Rule-of-thumb rates

| Daily deficit | Approx. loss per week |
|---|---|
| 250 kcal | 0.23 kg (0.5 lb) |
| 500 kcal | 0.45 kg (1 lb) |
| 750 kcal | 0.68 kg (1.5 lb) |
| 1,000 kcal | 0.91 kg (2 lb) |

These use the common estimate of 7,700 kcal per kilogram of body fat.

## Why the scale doesn't follow the maths exactly

- **Water weight**: early loss is often faster because stored carbohydrate holds water.
- **Metabolic adaptation**: as you lose weight, you need fewer calories, so the same intake gives a smaller deficit.
- **Daily swings**: salt, hormones and meals can move the scale by 1–2 kg from day to day.

Track the **weekly average** rather than single weigh-ins.

## Finding your deficit

1. Estimate your daily needs with the [BMR and TDEE calculator](/health/bmr/).
2. Subtract a moderate amount, usually 300–500 kcal.
3. Re-check every few weeks, because your TDEE drops as your weight does.

## Keeping it off

Most weight is regained when people return to old habits. Gradual changes you can live with — more vegetables and protein, fewer sugary drinks, regular activity and enough sleep — work better long term than strict diets.

## Important

- Very low-calorie diets should only be followed under medical supervision.
- Combine a moderate deficit with enough protein and regular activity to protect muscle.
- If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or have a history of eating difficulties, speak to a doctor or registered dietitian before changing your diet.
