    A z-score tells you how many standard deviations a value is above or below the mean. It lets you compare values from different data sets and find percentiles.

    How to use the Z-score calculator

    Enter the value, the mean and the standard deviation. The z-score and percentile appear instantly. To find the value at a given percentile, press × on Value and type the percentile.

    Z-score formula

    z = (x − μ) ÷ σ

    Worked example

    An exam score of 85 when the class mean is 70 and SD is 10:

    • z = (85 − 70) ÷ 10 = 1.5
    • Percentile = 93.3% — better than about 93% of the class

    Comparing scores on different tests

    Z-scores put different scales on a common footing:

    Test Your score Mean SD z
    Maths 78 65 10 +1.30
    English 85 80 4 +1.25

    Although the English score is higher, the maths result is slightly better relative to other students.

    Finding a raw score from z

    x = μ + z × σ. To reach the 90th percentile (z = 1.28) on a test with mean 70 and SD 10, you need 70 + 12.8 = 82.8.

    Z-scores in practice

    • Growth charts: children’s height and weight are reported as z-scores against WHO standards.
    • Bone density: T-scores and Z-scores compare bone density with reference populations.
    • Quality control: process measurements beyond ±3 are investigated.
    • Finance: the Altman Z-score combines ratios to assess bankruptcy risk (a different formula with the same name).

    Frequently asked questions

    What is a z-score?

    How many standard deviations a value is above or below the mean. z = 0 is exactly average; z = 2 is two SDs above.

    How do I find a percentile from a z-score?

    Use the normal distribution — the calculator gives the share of values below x, assuming the data is normally distributed.

    What z-score is unusual?

    Values beyond about ±2 occur roughly 5% of the time; beyond ±3, about 0.3% of the time.

    What does a negative z-score mean?

    The value is below the mean. A z of −1 is one standard deviation below.

    What z-score is the top 5%?

    About 1.645. Values above it are in the top 5% of a normal distribution.
