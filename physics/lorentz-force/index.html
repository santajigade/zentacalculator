    A charge moving through a magnetic field is pushed sideways, at right angles to both its velocity and the field, and a current carrying wire feels the same push. This Lorentz force calculator gives the force on a charge, its orbit radius and cyclotron frequency, and the force on a conductor.

    Physics students, motor and actuator designers and people working with mass spectrometers or beam lines use it to check forces, path radii and frequencies quickly.

    How to use the Lorentz force calculator

    For a moving charge, enter its charge number, speed, mass in atomic mass units, the magnetic flux density and the angle between velocity and field. You get the force, the radius of the circular (or helical) path and the cyclotron frequency. For a wire, enter the current and the length inside the field; the same B and angle are used.

    Lorentz force formulas

    F = |q| v B sin θ  ·  r = m v sin θ ÷ (|q| B)  ·  fc = |q| B ÷ (2π m)

    Conductor: F = B I L sin θ

    Example

    A proton (1.007276 u, charge +e) moving at 2 × 10⁶ m/s at right angles to a 0.5 T field:

    • F = 1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ × 2 × 10⁶ × 0.5 = 1.6022 × 10⁻¹³ N
    • r = 1.6726 × 10⁻²⁷ × 2 × 10⁶ ÷ (1.602 × 10⁻¹⁹ × 0.5) = 41.76 mm
    • fc = 7.623 MHz

    A 0.2 m wire carrying 5 A across the same field: F = 0.5 × 5 × 0.2 = 0.50 N.

    Cyclotron frequency and radius in a 1 T field

    The radius column is for a speed of 10⁶ m/s perpendicular to the field.

    Particle Charge Cyclotron frequency Radius at 10⁶ m/s
    Electron -1 2.799e+04 MHz 0.005686 mm
    Proton +1 15.25 MHz 10.44 mm
    Alpha particle +2 7.675 MHz 20.74 mm
    Deuteron +1 7.626 MHz 20.87 mm

    Notes

    • The formulas are non-relativistic; above about 10% of the speed of light use the relativistic momentum γmv.
    • A velocity component parallel to B is unaffected, so the particle spirals along the field lines.
    • Find the field of a coil or wire first with the magnetic field calculator.

    Default case taken further

    The default proton (2 × 10⁶ m/s across 0.5 T) gives F = 1.6022 × 10⁻¹³ N, r = 41.76 mm and fc = 7.623 MHz, and the 0.2 m wire with 5 A feels 0.50 N. Changing one thing at a time shows how the formulas behave:

    • Swap in an electron (0.00054858 u, charge −1) at the same speed: the force has the same size but the opposite direction, the radius shrinks to 22.7 µm and the cyclotron frequency rises to 14.0 GHz.
    • Tilt the velocity to 30° from the field: the force halves to 8.0109 × 10⁻¹⁴ N and the circle radius halves to 20.88 mm, while the particle drifts along B by 227 mm per turn, tracing a helix.
    • Double the speed: the force and the radius double, but the cyclotron frequency does not change, which is what lets a cyclotron use a fixed RF frequency.

    Mass spectrometer example

    Singly charged carbon ions of 12 u and 13.00335 u enter a 0.5 T field at 1 × 10⁵ m/s. Their radii are 24.87 mm and 26.95 mm, so after a half circle they land 4.16 mm apart on the detector. Heavier ions bend less, which is how isotopes are separated and identified.

    Force on a 1 m conductor

    Current Earth field, 50 µT 0.1 T 0.5 T 1.5 T
    1 A 5e-05 N 0.1 N 0.5 N 1.5 N
    10 A 0.0005 N 1 N 5 N 15 N
    100 A 0.005 N 10 N 50 N 150 N
    1,000 A 0.05 N 100 N 500 N 1.5e+03 N

    Fields of 0.5 T to 1.5 T are typical of the air gap in motors and loudspeakers; the Earth’s field produces only tiny forces even on heavy cables.

    Common mistakes

    • Measuring the angle from the wrong reference. θ is between the velocity (or current) and the field; motion parallel to B gives zero force.
    • Entering mass in kilograms. The mass field is in atomic mass units, so a proton is 1.007276, not 1.67 × 10⁻²⁷.
    • Forgetting the sign of the charge. The size of the force uses |q|, but an electron is pushed the opposite way to a proton.
    • Using a length of wire outside the field. Only the length actually inside the field region counts in F = B I L.
    • Applying it near light speed. Above about 0.1c use the relativistic momentum, or the radius comes out too small.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the Lorentz force formula?

    F = q(E + v × B). With no electric field the magnitude is F = |q| v B sin θ, where θ is the angle between the velocity and the magnetic field.

    What is the force on a current-carrying wire?

    F = B I L sin θ. A 0.2 m conductor carrying 5 A at right angles to a 0.5 T field feels 0.5 N — the basic force in every electric motor.

    Why do charged particles move in circles in a magnetic field?

    The magnetic force is always perpendicular to the velocity, so it changes the direction but not the speed. The radius is r = m v⊥ ÷ (|q| B).

    Which way does the force act?

    Use the right-hand rule for positive charges (or conventional current) — fingers along v, curl towards B, thumb gives F. The force on an electron is in the opposite direction.

    Does the magnetic force do work on a moving charge?

    No. The force is always perpendicular to the velocity, so it changes the direction of motion but not the speed or kinetic energy. Only an electric field can speed a charge up.

    What is the cyclotron frequency of an electron in the Earth's magnetic field?

    About 1.4 MHz in a 50 µT field. Electrons spiralling around field lines in the upper atmosphere emit and absorb radio waves near this frequency.
