## How to use this calculator

Enter the **home price**, **down payment %**, **interest rate** and **term**. You get the loan amount, monthly payment and total interest. Work backwards to find the price you can afford or the down payment needed.

## The formula

<p class="formula">M = P × i × (1 + i)<sup>n</sup> ÷ ((1 + i)<sup>n</sup> − 1)</p>

## Worked example

A 400,000 home with 20% down, 6.5% for 30 years:

- Loan = 400,000 × 0.8 = **320,000**
- Monthly payment = **2,022.62**
- Total interest over 30 years = **408,142**

## 15 vs 20 vs 30 years

For 320,000 at 6.5%:

| Term | Monthly payment | Total interest |
|---|---|---|
| 15 years | 2,787.54 | 181,758 |
| 20 years | 2,385.83 | 252,600 |
| 30 years | 2,022.62 | 408,142 |

A 15-year loan costs about 765 more each month but saves over 226,000 in interest.

## How the rate changes the payment

Loan of 320,000 over 30 years:

| Rate | Monthly payment | Total interest |
|---|---|---|
| 5% | 1,717.83 | 298,419 |
| 5.5% | 1,816.92 | 334,093 |
| 6% | 1,918.56 | 370,682 |
| 6.5% | 2,022.62 | 408,142 |
| 7% | 2,128.97 | 446,428 |
| 7.5% | 2,237.49 | 485,495 |

Each half-point change in rate moves the payment by roughly 100 a month on this loan and the total interest by tens of thousands.

## The full monthly cost of owning

The calculator shows principal and interest only. A typical full payment (often called **PITI**) also includes:

- **Property tax**, often 0.5–2% of the home value a year.
- **Home insurance**.
- **Mortgage insurance** if your down payment is below 20%.
- **HOA or maintenance fees** for flats and gated communities.

Add these to the result to see your real monthly cost.

## How much income do you need?

Many lenders use the **28/36 rule**: housing costs no more than 28% of gross monthly income, and all debt payments no more than 36%. For a principal-and-interest payment of 2,023 plus about 500 of tax and insurance, you would need a gross income of roughly 9,009 a month.

## Extra payments

Paying one extra monthly payment a year on a 30-year loan at 6.5% shortens it by about 5–6 years and saves a large share of the interest. Check that your lender has no prepayment penalty first.

## Ways to pay less

- **Bigger down payment** — reduces the loan and often the rate.
- **Extra payments** — even one extra payment a year can cut years off a 30-year loan.
- **Shop for the rate** — 0.5% less on 320,000 over 30 years saves about 37,500.
