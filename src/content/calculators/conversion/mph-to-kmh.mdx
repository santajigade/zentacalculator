## How to use this converter

Type a value in **Miles per hour** and the other units update instantly. Every field is live, so you can just as easily start from kilometres per hour or any other unit and convert back.

## The formula

<p class="formula">km/h = mph × 1.609344</p>

1 mph = 1.60934 km/h, and 1 km/h = 0.621371 mph.

## Definitions

- **Miles per hour (mph)**: used for road speeds in the US and UK.
- **Kilometres per hour (km/h)**: used in most other countries.
- **Knot**: one nautical mile per hour (1.852 km/h), used at sea and in aviation.

## Stopping distance grows fast

Doubling speed roughly quadruples braking distance. At 60 mph (97 km/h), total stopping distance is about 73 m in good conditions. See the [SUVAT calculator](/physics/suvat-equations/).

## Quick estimate

Multiply mph by 1.6: 50 mph × 1.6 = 80 km/h (exact 80.5).
