## How to use this calculator

Enter your **waist** and **hip** measurements. The ratio appears instantly — compare it with the table below for your sex.

## The formula

<p class="formula">WHR = waist ÷ hip</p>

## Worked example

Waist 85 cm, hips 95 cm: WHR = 85 ÷ 95 = **0.895**. For a man this is just under the WHO threshold of 0.90; for a woman it would be above 0.85.

## WHO thresholds

| | Low risk | Increased risk |
|---|---|---|
| Men | ≤ 0.90 | > 0.90 |
| Women | ≤ 0.85 | > 0.85 |

Source: World Health Organization, *Waist circumference and waist–hip ratio* (2008).

## How to measure

- **Waist:** midway between the lowest rib and the top of the hip bone, after a normal breath out.
- **Hips:** around the widest part of the buttocks.
- Stand relaxed with feet together; keep the tape snug but not compressing the skin.

This is a screening tool, not a diagnosis.
