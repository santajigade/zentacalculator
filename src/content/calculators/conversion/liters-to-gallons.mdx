## How to use this converter

Type a value in **litres**, **US gallons** or **UK gallons** — the other two update instantly.

## Formula

<p class="formula">US gal = L ÷ 3.78541   UK gal = L ÷ 4.54609</p>

**Example:** filling a 50-litre fuel tank = **13.2 US gallons** = **11.0 UK gallons**.

## US vs UK gallons

- **US gallon** = 3.785 L, used in the United States.
- **UK (imperial) gallon** = 4.546 L, used in the UK for fuel economy and some liquids.

A UK gallon is about 20% larger, which is why the same car has a higher mpg figure in the UK than in the US.

## Fuel economy conversions

- **km/L to US mpg:** multiply by 2.352
- **L/100 km to US mpg:** 235.2 ÷ (L/100 km)
- **US mpg to UK mpg:** multiply by 1.201

Example: a car using 6 L/100 km does about 39.2 US mpg or 47.1 UK mpg.

## Common container sizes

A standard oil drum holds 55 US gallons (about 208 L). A typical IBC tote holds 1,000 L (about 264 US gallons).
