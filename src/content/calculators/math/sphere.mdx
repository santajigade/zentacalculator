## How to use this calculator

Type **any one** of radius, diameter, volume or surface area — the others are calculated. Choose litres or gallons as the volume unit for tanks.

## Formulas

<p class="formula">V = ⁴⁄₃ π r³   S = 4π r²</p>

## Worked example

A ball with a 6 cm radius:

- Volume = 4/3 × π × 6³ = **904.8 cm³** (0.905 litres)
- Surface area = 4 × π × 6² = **452.4 cm²**

## Volume grows fast

Volume depends on the **cube** of the radius. Doubling the radius makes the sphere **8 times** heavier and larger in volume, while surface area grows only 4 times. This is why large spherical tanks store far more per square metre of steel.

## Hemisphere

A hemisphere (half a sphere) has volume ⅔πr³ — half the result above. Its curved surface is 2πr², plus a flat base of πr².
