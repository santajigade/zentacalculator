## How to use this converter

Type a value in **feet** or **metres** and the rest update instantly. For a height like 5 ft 9 in, type it in inches (69) or as decimal feet (5.75).

## Formula

<p class="formula">metres = feet × 0.3048   feet = metres ÷ 0.3048</p>

**Example:** 6 ft = 6 × 0.3048 = **1.829 m** (182.9 cm). A ceiling height of 2.7 m is 2.7 ÷ 0.3048 = **8.86 ft**.
