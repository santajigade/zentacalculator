Type a value in **Kilowatts** and the other units update instantly. Every field is live, so you can just as easily start from horsepower (mechanical) or any other unit and convert back.

## KW to HP formula

<p class="formula">hp = kW ÷ 0.7457 ≈ kW × 1.341</p>

1 kW = 1.34102 hp, and 1 hp = 0.7457 kW.

## Definitions

- **Mechanical horsepower (hp)**: 745.7 W. Used in the US and on many motor nameplates.
- **Metric horsepower (PS, CV)**: 735.5 W. Common in European and Asian car specifications.
- **Kilowatt**: 1,000 W, the SI unit used on IEC motor ratings.

Labels are rounded; 7.5 kW is exactly 10.06 hp.

## Electrical input vs output

A motor's kW or hp rating is its **shaft output**. Electrical input is higher because of losses: a 7.5 kW motor at 90% efficiency draws about 8.3 kW. See the [three-phase motor power calculator](/electrical/three-phase-motor-power/).

## Car engines

Car brochures may use kW, hp or PS. 100 kW = 134 hp = 136 PS.
