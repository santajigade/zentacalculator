## How to use the feet to meters converter

Type a value in **feet** or **metres** and the rest update instantly. For a height like 5 ft 9 in, type it in inches (69) or as decimal feet (5.75).

## Feet to meters formula

<p class="formula">metres = feet × 0.3048   feet = metres ÷ 0.3048</p>

**Example:** 6 ft = 6 × 0.3048 = **1.829 m** (182.9 cm). A ceiling height of 2.7 m is 2.7 ÷ 0.3048 = **8.86 ft**.

## Metres to feet

Multiply metres by 3.2808 to get feet. 1.75 m = 5.74 ft. To split into feet and inches: 0.74 ft × 12 = 8.9 in, so **5 ft 9 in**.

## Altitude and building heights

| Example | Feet | Metres |
|---|---|---|
| One storey of a house | about 10 | about 3 |
| Typical cruising altitude | 35,000 | 10,668 |

Aviation uses feet for altitude almost everywhere, while most maps use metres.

## Square feet are different

For areas, 1 m² = 10.764 ft² — not 3.28. Use the [square feet to square metres converter](/conversion/sq-ft-to-sq-m/).
