## How to use this calculator

Enter the **base radius** and **height** to get the slant height, volume and surface areas. You can also solve for the height from a volume, or the radius from the slant height.

## Formulas

<p class="formula">s = √(r² + h²)   V = ⅓πr²h<br/>Lateral area = πrs   Total = πrs + πr²</p>

## Worked example

A cone with a 3 cm radius and 4 cm height:

- Slant height = √(9 + 16) = **5 cm**
- Volume = ⅓ × π × 9 × 4 = **37.7 cm³**
- Lateral area = π × 3 × 5 = **47.1 cm²**
- Total surface = 47.1 + 28.3 = **75.4 cm²**

## Practical uses

- **Silos and hoppers** have conical bottoms — add the cone volume to the cylinder above.
- **Piles of sand or gravel** form cones. Measure the base circumference and height to estimate the volume.
- **Funnels and paper cups** use the lateral area to work out how much material is needed.
