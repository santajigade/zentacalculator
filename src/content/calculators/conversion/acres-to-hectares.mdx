## Acres to hectares formula

<p class="formula">hectares = acres × 0.404686   acres = hectares × 2.47105</p>

**Example:** a 10-acre farm is 10 × 0.404686 = **4.05 hectares**. A 3-hectare field is **7.41 acres**.

## Definitions

- **Acre**: exactly 4,046.8564224 m², or 43,560 square feet. Historically the area a team of oxen could plough in a day.
- **Hectare**: exactly 10,000 m², a square 100 m × 100 m. It is the standard land unit in the metric system.
- **1 hectare = 2.4711 acres**, and **1 acre = 0.4047 hectares**.

## How big is an acre?

An acre is 43,560 ft² or about 4,047 m² — about 57% of a full-size (105 × 68 m) football pitch, or a square about 63.6 m on each side. A hectare is a 100 m × 100 m square.

## Where each is used

Hectares are the international standard for agriculture and forestry. Acres remain common in the USA, the UK, Canada, Australia and India.
