## How to use this converter

Enter a **numerator** and **denominator** to get the decimal and percentage. You can also type a decimal or percentage with a known denominator to find the numerator — for example, how many sixteenths make 0.4375 (answer: 7).

## Formula

<p class="formula">decimal = numerator ÷ denominator   percent = decimal × 100</p>

## Inch fractions

Imperial drawings use fractions of an inch: 3/8″ = 0.375″ = 9.525 mm. Halves, quarters, eighths and sixteenths always give exact (terminating) decimals because their denominators are powers of 2.
