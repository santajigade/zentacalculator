## How to use this calculator

Enter your **balance**, **APR** and the **fixed monthly payment** you can make. The calculator shows how many months it will take and the total interest. To set a deadline, press × on Monthly payment and type the months.

This assumes no new purchases on the card.

## The formula

<p class="formula">Balance = Payment × (1 − (1 + i)<sup>−n</sup>) ÷ i   (i = APR ÷ 12)</p>

## Worked example

Balance 5,000 at 20% APR, paying 200 a month:

- Time to pay off = **32.6 months**
- Total interest = **1,522**

## Paying more saves a lot

For 5,000 at 20% APR:

| Monthly payment | Months | Total interest |
|---|---|---|
| 150 | 49.1 | 2,359 |
| 200 | 32.6 | 1,522 |
| 300 | 19.7 | 906 |
| 500 | 11.0 | 515 |

## Strategies

- **Avalanche:** pay extra on the highest-APR card first — saves the most interest.
- **Snowball:** clear the smallest balance first — quick wins keep you motivated.
- **Balance transfer:** moving to a 0% card can help, but watch fees and the rate after the offer ends.
