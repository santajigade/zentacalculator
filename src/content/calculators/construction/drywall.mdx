## How to use the drywall / plasterboard calculator

Enter the total **area** of walls and ceilings and choose the **sheet size**. The number of sheets with waste is calculated.

## Example

60 m² with 2400 × 1200 mm sheets and 10% waste:

60 × 1.1 ÷ 2.88 = **23 sheets**

## Sheets needed by room size

Walls only, 2.4 m ceiling, 1.2 × 2.4 m sheets (2.88 m²), 10% waste:

| Room | Wall area (approx.) | Sheets |
|---|---|---|
| 3 × 3 m | 28.8 m² | 11 |
| 3 × 4 m | 33.6 m² | 13 |
| 4 × 4 m | 38.4 m² | 15 |
| 4 × 5 m | 43.2 m² | 17 |
| 5 × 6 m | 52.8 m² | 21 |

Add the ceiling area (length × width) if you are boarding that too.

## Standard sheet sizes

| Size | Area |

| 1200 × 2700 mm | 3.24 m² |

Longer sheets reduce joints on tall walls and ceilings.

## Other materials

| Material | Rough quantity per 100 m² of board |
|---|---|

## Board types

- **Standard**: dry areas.
- **Moisture-resistant** (often green): bathrooms and kitchens.
- **Fire-rated** (often pink or red): garages, escape routes and where codes require.
- **Sound-rated**: party walls and bedrooms.

## Measuring walls

Wall area = room perimeter × wall height. For a 5 × 4 m room with 2.4 m walls: 18 × 2.4 = 43.2 m², plus the ceiling of 20 m².
