## How to use the KM to miles converter

Type a distance in **kilometres** or **miles** (or metres, or nautical miles) and the other units update instantly.

## KM to miles formula

<p class="formula">miles = km × 0.621371   km = miles × 1.609344</p>

**Example:** a 10 km run is 10 × 0.621371 = **6.21 miles**. A 26.2-mile marathon is 26.2 × 1.609344 = **42.2 km**.

## Definition

One international mile is **exactly 1,609.344 metres** (5,280 feet). One kilometre is 0.621371 miles.

## Mental trick

Use the Fibonacci numbers: 3 miles ≈ 5 km, 5 miles ≈ 8 km, 8 miles ≈ 13 km. Or multiply km by 0.6 for a quick estimate.

## Nautical miles

Sea and air navigation use the nautical mile (1,852 m), based on one minute of latitude. Speed in nautical miles per hour is a knot.
