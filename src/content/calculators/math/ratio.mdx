## How to use this calculator

The calculator solves **A : B = C : D**. Enter any **three** values and the fourth is found. To change which value is calculated, press × on it.

## The formula

<p class="formula">A ÷ B = C ÷ D   →   A × D = B × C</p>

## Worked examples

**Scaling a recipe.** A recipe uses 3 cups of flour for 4 servings. How much for 20 servings?
3 : 4 = C : 20 → C = 3 × 20 ÷ 4 = **15 cups**

**Map scale.** On a 1 : 50,000 map, 6 cm on paper is 1 : 50,000 = 6 : D → D = 300,000 cm = **3 km**.

**Mixing.** Concrete at 1 : 2 cement to sand needs 2 × 50 = 100 kg of sand for a 50 kg bag of cement.

## Direct vs inverse proportion

This calculator is for **direct** proportion (both values grow together). For **inverse** proportion — such as more workers finishing a job in fewer days — multiply instead: 4 workers × 10 days = 5 workers × 8 days.
