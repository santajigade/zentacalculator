## How to use the CGPA to percentage calculator

1. Enter your **CGPA** on a 10-point scale.
2. Choose the **conversion formula** your board or university uses.

The percentage appears instantly. To go from percentage to CGPA, press × on CGPA and type the percentage.

## Conversion formulas

| Board / university | Formula |
|---|---|
| CBSE (Class 10, older CCE scheme) | % = CGPA × 9.5 |
| AICTE recommended (engineering) | % = (CGPA − 0.75) × 10 |
| University of Mumbai (10-point scale) | % = 7.1 × CGPA + 11 |
| Several universities (simple) | % = CGPA × 10 |

Universities set their own rules, and some publish a different formula or a conversion table. Always check your marksheet or university notice.

## SGPA vs CGPA

- **SGPA** (semester grade point average) covers a single semester.
- **CGPA** (cumulative) is the credit-weighted average of all SGPAs so far:

<p class="formula">CGPA = Σ(SGPA × semester credits) ÷ Σ(credits)</p>

If credits are equal each semester, CGPA is simply the average of the SGPAs.

## Why CBSE used 9.5

CBSE derived 9.5 from the average percentage of students scoring in the top grade band across subjects. It applied to the CCE grading scheme; check current CBSE marksheets for how results are reported now.

## Applying abroad

Universities abroad may ask for a WES or similar credential evaluation, which converts grades using its own method. Keep your official transcripts and the university's conversion certificate.

## Worked example: CGPA 8.2 with each formula

The default CGPA of 8.2 gives a different answer under each rule, which is why the formula matters more than the number:

| Formula | Calculation | Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| CBSE | 8.2 × 9.5 | **77.9%** |
| AICTE | (8.2 − 0.75) × 10 | **74.5%** |
| Mumbai University | 7.1 × 8.2 + 11 | **69.22%** |
| Simple | 8.2 × 10 | **82%** |

The spread between the lowest and highest result is 12.8 percentage points, enough to cross a 75% or 80% eligibility line.

## From SGPA to CGPA to percentage

An engineering student has four semesters: SGPA 7.8 (22 credits), 8.4 (24), 8.1 (20) and 8.9 (24). The credit-weighted CGPA is (7.8 × 22 + 8.4 × 24 + 8.1 × 20 + 8.9 × 24) ÷ 90 = **8.32**. A plain average of the four SGPAs gives 8.30, slightly off because the semesters carry different credits. Under the AICTE rule, 8.32 becomes (8.32 − 0.75) × 10 = **75.7%**.

## When you are asked for a percentage

Check the bottom of your marksheet or the university's grading notice first: many universities print their own conversion and will issue a conversion certificate on request. CBSE returned to marks for Class 10 board results from 2018, so the 9.5 rule applies to older CCE marksheets. When a form asks for a percentage and your institution has no published rule, state the CGPA as well and name the formula you used, so nobody thinks you inflated the figure.

## Common mistakes

- **Using CBSE's 9.5 for a university CGPA.** It was set for the CBSE CCE scheme only; engineering colleges under AICTE use (CGPA − 0.75) × 10.
- **Averaging SGPAs without credits.** Weight each semester by its credits, as in the example above.
- **Converting a 4-point GPA with a 10-point formula.** Convert to the correct scale first, or use the evaluator's own method for study abroad.
- **Rounding the CGPA before converting.** 8.249 rounded to 8.2 loses almost half a percentage point under the CBSE rule.
- **Quoting a converted figure as official.** Unless your marksheet or a university certificate states it, call it an equivalent percentage.
