    Frequently asked questions

    Does the age calculator count leap years?

    Yes. It uses the real calendar, so leap days and months of different lengths are counted correctly.

    How do I find the running cost of an appliance?

    Multiply its power in kW by the hours used and by your tariff per kWh. A 1.5 kW AC running 8 hours at ₹8 per unit costs about ₹96 a day.

    Is the CGPA to percentage formula the same for every university?

    No. CBSE uses CGPA × 9.5, but many universities publish their own formula. Check your marksheet or university rules.
