    Million, lakh and crore: the two numbering systems

    The international system groups digits in threes (thousand, million, billion). The Indian system groups the first three digits, then pairs (thousand, lakh, crore). The same number therefore gets commas in different places:

    Number International Indian
    1,00,000 100 thousand 1 lakh
    10,00,000 1 million 10 lakh
    1,00,00,000 10 million 1 crore
    1,00,00,00,000 1 billion 100 crore

    Number to million formulas

    millions = number ÷ 1,000,000
    lakhs = number ÷ 100,000
    crores = number ÷ 10,000,000

    Quick mental shortcuts:

    • Million → lakh: multiply by 10 (3.2 million = 32 lakh).
    • Crore → million: multiply by 10 (4.5 crore = 45 million).
    • Billion → crore: multiply by 100 (1.2 billion = 120 crore).

    Worked example

    A company reports revenue of ₹ 845 crore. In international terms:

    845 crore × 10,000,000 = 8,450,000,000 → 8.45 billion, or 8,450 million.

    Short scale vs long scale

    Modern English uses the short scale, where a billion is 1,000 million (109). Some European languages use the long scale, where a billion (milliard in some languages) means a million million (1012). Check the context when reading older British or European texts.

    Reading large numbers in reports

    Company results in India are usually reported in crore, while global reports use million and billion. Converting ₹45,000 crore: 45,000 × 10 million = 450 billion rupees.

    Where this conversion matters

    • Reading Indian financial results next to global reports.
    • Writing for international audiences when the source figures are in lakh/crore.
    • Checking population, budget or valuation figures quoted in different systems.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many lakhs are in one million?

    One million equals 10 lakh, because 1,000,000 ÷ 100,000 = 10.

    How many millions are in one crore?

    One crore equals 10 million (1,00,00,000 = 10,000,000).

    How do I write 2.5 million in numbers?

    Multiply by 1,000,000. 2.5 million = 2,500,000, which is 25 lakh in the Indian system.

    Is a billion 100 crore?

    Yes. One billion is 1,000,000,000, which equals 100 crore or 10,000 lakh.

    How many lakhs make a million?

    10 lakh = 1 million.

    How many crores make a billion?

    100 crore = 1 billion.

    How many million is 4.5 crore?

    45 million (45,000,000), because 1 crore equals 10 million.

    How many zeros are in a million?

    Six: 1,000,000. A billion has nine zeros and a crore has seven.

    How do you write 23.4 million in numbers?

    23,400,000 — 23.4 × 1,000,000. In India that is 2.34 crore (2,34,00,000).

    How do you write 3.2 million in numbers?

    3,200,000, which is 32 lakh.
