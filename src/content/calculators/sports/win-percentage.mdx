## How to use this calculator

Enter **wins**, **losses** and **ties**. Win percentage and win–loss ratio are calculated.

## Formula

<p class="formula">Win % = (W + T/2) ÷ (W + L + T)</p>

## Example

42 wins, 28 losses, 2 ties: (42 + 1) ÷ 72 = **59.7%** (.597).
