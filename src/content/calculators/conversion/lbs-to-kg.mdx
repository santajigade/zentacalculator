## Pounds to kilograms formula

<p class="formula">kg = lb × 0.45359237</p>

1 lb = 0.453592 kg, and 1 kg = 2.20462 lb.

## Definitions

- **Pound (lb)**: exactly 0.45359237 kg (international pound, 1959). 1 lb = 16 oz.
- **Kilogram**: the SI base unit of mass, about 2.20462 lb.

## Gym plates

Plates are sold in kg or lb: a 20 kg plate is 44.1 lb, and a 45 lb plate is 20.4 kg. A standard Olympic bar is 20 kg (44 lb).

## Mental shortcut

Divide pounds by 2.2: 150 lb ÷ 2.2 ≈ 68 kg. For a quick estimate, halve and take away 10%: 150 → 75 → 67.5 kg.
