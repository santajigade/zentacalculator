## How to use this calculator

Enter the **dynamic pressure** read from the manometer, the **fluid density** and the pitot **coefficient**. The velocity appears straight away. You can also work backwards to find the pressure you should expect for a target velocity.

## The formula

<p class="formula">v = C × √(2ΔP ÷ ρ)</p>

## Worked example

Air at 20 °C (ρ = 1.2 kg/m³), pitot reading 500 Pa (5 mbar):

v = √(2 × 500 ÷ 1.2) = **28.9 m/s**

## Measuring tips

- Point the tip straight into the flow, within about ±10°.
- Velocity varies across a duct. For flow measurement, take a **traverse** — several readings at standard positions — and average the velocities (not the pressures).
- Below about 3 m/s in air, the pressure is very small and readings become unreliable.
