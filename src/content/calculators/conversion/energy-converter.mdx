## How to use this converter

Type an energy value in **any** unit and the others update instantly — useful for electricity bills, food labels, heating and engineering.

## Formula

<p class="formula">1 kWh = 3.6 MJ = 3,412 BTU   1 kcal = 4.184 kJ   1 therm = 100,000 BTU</p>

**Example:** **1 kWh** = 3,600 kJ = 860 kcal = 3,412 BTU. A 2,000 kcal daily diet is about **2.3 kWh** of energy.

## Food calories

On food labels, "Calories" with a capital C are kilocalories (kcal). Many countries also print kJ: 1 kcal = 4.184 kJ.

## Energy vs power

**Energy** (kWh, J) is an amount. **Power** (kW, W) is the rate of using it. A 2 kW heater running for 3 hours uses 2 × 3 = **6 kWh**. Use the [power converter](/conversion/power-converter/) for kW, hp and BTU/h.
