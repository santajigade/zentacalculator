## How to use this calculator

Enter the **amount invested**, the **final value** and the **holding period**. You get the net gain, total ROI and annualized ROI. Work backwards to see the final value needed for a target return.

## Formulas

<p class="formula">ROI = (F − I) ÷ I<br/>Annualized ROI = (F ÷ I)<sup>1/t</sup> − 1</p>

## Worked example

10,000 invested, worth 15,000 after 3 years:

- Net gain = **5,000**
- Total ROI = 5,000 ÷ 10,000 = **50%**
- Annualized ROI = 1.5<sup>1/3</sup> − 1 = **14.47% per year**

## ROI for business decisions

ROI is also used to compare projects. A marketing campaign costing 2,000 that brings in 2,600 of extra profit has an ROI of 30%. Compare projects by **annualized** ROI when they last different lengths of time.

## Limits

- ROI ignores risk — a high ROI may come with high chance of loss.
- It ignores the timing of cash flows within the period. For projects with many cash flows, use NPV or IRR.
