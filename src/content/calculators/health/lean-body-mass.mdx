## How to use this calculator

Choose your **sex** and enter **height** and **weight**. Lean body mass and the implied body fat percentage appear instantly.

## The Boer formula (1984)

<p class="formula">Men: LBM = 0.407 × W + 0.267 × H − 19.2<br/>Women: LBM = 0.252 × W + 0.473 × H − 48.3</p>

W = weight in kg, H = height in cm.

## Worked example

A man 180 cm and 80 kg:

LBM = 0.407 × 80 + 0.267 × 180 − 19.2 = **61.4 kg** → about 23% body fat

A woman 165 cm and 60 kg: LBM = **44.9 kg**.

## Using your result

- **Protein target:** many athletes aim for about 2 g per kg of lean mass.
- **Tracking progress:** if weight drops but lean mass holds steady, you are losing fat rather than muscle.

For a measurement-based estimate, try the [body fat calculator](/health/body-fat/).
