## How to use this calculator

Enter the **volume** of the drink and its **ABV** (alcohol by volume, shown on the label). You get UK units and US standard drinks.

## Formulas

<p class="formula">UK units = volume (ml) × ABV% ÷ 1000<br/>US standard drinks = volume (ml) × ABV ÷ 17.74</p>

One UK unit = 10 ml (8 g) of pure alcohol. One US standard drink = about 17.7 ml (14 g).

## Worked example

A 500 ml bottle of 5% beer:

- UK units = 500 × 5 ÷ 1000 = **2.5 units**
- US standard drinks = 500 × 0.05 ÷ 17.74 = **1.41 drinks**
