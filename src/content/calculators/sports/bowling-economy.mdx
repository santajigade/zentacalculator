## How to use this calculator

Enter **runs conceded**, **wickets** and **balls bowled**. Economy, average and strike rate are calculated.

## Example

480 runs, 20 wickets, 432 balls (72 overs):

- Economy = **6.67**
- Average = **24.00**
- Strike rate = **21.6** balls per wicket
