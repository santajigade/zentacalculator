## How to use this calculator

Enter your **waist** and **height** in the same or different units. Your ratio and risk category appear instantly. To find the maximum healthy waist for your height, press × on Waist and type 0.5 as the ratio.

## The formula

<p class="formula">WHtR = waist ÷ height</p>

## Worked example

Waist 85 cm, height 178 cm:

WHtR = 85 ÷ 178 = **0.478** → healthy (below 0.5)

The maximum healthy waist for this height is 178 × 0.5 = 89 cm.

## Categories

| WHtR | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Below 0.4 | Take care — may be underweight |
| 0.4 – 0.49 | Healthy |
| 0.5 – 0.59 | Increased health risk |
| 0.6 and above | High health risk |

UK NICE guidance (2022) recommends keeping waist size to less than half of height.

This is a screening tool. Speak to a doctor about your individual health.
