## Definitions

- **Guntha (gunta)**: 1/40 of an acre — 1,089 sq ft (33 ft × 33 ft) or 101.17 m².
- **Acre**: 40 guntha, or 43,560 sq ft.
- **Are** (in some records): 100 m², close to one guntha.

## Where guntha is used

Guntha appears on 7/12 extracts and other land records in Maharashtra, in Karnataka and Telangana (as gunta) and in parts of Gujarat. It is also common in property listings for farm and NA plots.

## Old measuring chain

The guntha comes from the surveyor's chain of 33 feet: one guntha is one square chain of 33 × 33 ft.

## Worked example: acres and gunthas on a land record

Land records often write area as acres plus gunthas, for example **2 acres 15 guntha**:

- Total gunthas = 2 × 40 + 15 = **95 guntha**
- Square feet = 95 × 1,089 = **103,455 sq ft**
- Square metres = 103,455 × 0.09290304 = **9,611.3 m²**, or 0.9611 ha

Going the other way, a 3,000 sq ft house plot is 3,000 ÷ 1,089 = **2.755 guntha**.

## Measuring a plot in practice

Government surveyors now measure in metres with a total station or GPS rover, and the area is converted to guntha or hectares for the record. A rectangular plot of 40 m × 25.3 m covers 1,012 m², which is 10.00 guntha. Irregular plots are split into triangles, each worked out from its three sides, and the parts added. Before a sale, compare the measured area with the record and the layout plan; small differences of a few square metres are common, but a gap of a guntha or more needs a fresh survey.

## Common mistakes

- **Reading 1 acre 20 guntha as 1.2 acres.** Gunthas are fortieths of an acre, so 20 guntha is half an acre and the total is 1.5 acres.
- **Treating a guntha as 1,000 sq ft.** The shortcut understates the area by 8.2%, which matters when land is priced per square foot.
- **Mixing up guntha and are.** They are close but not equal: 1 guntha is 101.17 m² and 1 are is exactly 100 m².
- **Confusing guntha with the Tamil Nadu ground.** A ground is 2,400 sq ft, more than twice a guntha.
- **Using square yards and square feet interchangeably.** One guntha is 121 sq yd (gaj) but 1,089 sq ft; a factor of 9 separates them.
