## The formula

<p class="formula">sq ft = m² × 10.7639</p>

1 m² = 10.7639 sq ft, and 1 sq ft = 0.092903 m².

## Definitions

- **Square metre**: the area of a square 1 m on each side.
- **Square foot**: the area of a square 1 ft on each side, 0.0929 m². 1 m² = 10.764 sq ft.

## Why not multiply by 3.28?

A square metre is 3.28 ft **by** 3.28 ft, so it equals 3.28 × 3.28 = 10.76 sq ft. Multiplying by 3.28 is a common mistake that undercounts area by more than two-thirds.
