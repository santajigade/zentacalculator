## How to use this calculator

Enter your **systolic** (top) and **diastolic** (bottom) blood pressure in mmHg. You get the pulse pressure and the mean arterial pressure.

## Formulas

<p class="formula">Pulse pressure = SBP − DBP<br/>MAP ≈ DBP + (SBP − DBP) ÷ 3</p>

## Worked example

Blood pressure 120/80 mmHg:

- Pulse pressure = 40 mmHg
- MAP = 80 + 40 ÷ 3 = **93.3 mmHg**

## Blood pressure categories (adults)

| Category | Systolic | | Diastolic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Normal | below 120 | and | below 80 |
| Elevated | 120–129 | and | below 80 |
| High — stage 1 | 130–139 | or | 80–89 |
| High — stage 2 | 140 or higher | or | 90 or higher |

Source: American Heart Association / American College of Cardiology (2017). Other countries use slightly different thresholds.

Seek urgent medical care for a reading above 180/120 mmHg with symptoms such as chest pain, shortness of breath or vision changes. This calculator is for information only.
