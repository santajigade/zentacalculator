## How to use the PSI to bar converter

Type a value in **psi**, **bar** or **kPa** and the others update instantly.

## PSI to bar formula

<p class="formula">bar = psi × 0.0689476   psi = bar × 14.5038</p>

**Example:** a car tyre at 32 psi is **2.21 bar** (221 kPa). A 10 bar compressor delivers **145 psi**.

## Tyre pressures

Car tyres usually run at about 30–36 psi (2.1–2.5 bar). Check the label in your door frame or the owner's manual, and measure when the tyres are cold.

## psi vs psig vs psia

- **psig** (gauge) is pressure above atmospheric. Tyre gauges read psig.
- **psia** (absolute) includes atmospheric pressure: psia = psig + 14.7 at sea level.
- The same applies to bar(g) and bar(a).

## Quick estimate

Divide psi by 14.5 to get bar, or multiply bar by 14.5 to get psi. Roughly, **1 bar ≈ 14.5 psi** and **100 psi ≈ 7 bar**.
