    Tech and digital tools

    The download time calculator shows how long a file takes at your internet speed, remembering that speeds are sold in megabits (Mbps) while files are measured in megabytes (MB), a factor of 8. Convert between data storage units, estimate video file size from bitrate, and check screen PPI or the largest sharp print size for a photo. The password strength checker estimates how long a password would take to guess.

    Home and pets

    Estimate how long an LPG cylinder will last, how long a water heater takes to heat a tank, or the volume of an aquarium. Pet owners can convert dog years and cat years into human years with newer, research-based formulas rather than the old "× 7" rule, and set daily dog food calories.

    Family planning

    The due date calculator estimates the delivery date from the last period, conception or IVF transfer date, and the ovulation calculator shows the fertile window. These are estimates only; always follow the dates given by your doctor or midwife.

    Frequently asked questions

    Why does my download take longer than the calculator says?

    The calculator uses your full line speed. Real downloads are slower because of Wi-Fi, server limits and protocol overhead, often by 10% to 30%.

    Is one dog year really seven human years?

    No. Dogs age fastest in their first year or two, then more slowly. The dog age calculator uses a DNA-based formula (16 × ln(dog age) + 31) and shows the old × 7 figure next to it for comparison.

    Which LPG cylinder size does the calculator use?

    It starts with the 14.2 kg domestic cylinder, but you can enter any size, such as 5 kg or a 19 kg commercial cylinder.
