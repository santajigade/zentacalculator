## How to use the wallpaper calculator

Enter the **wall length** to cover, **wall height**, **roll width and length**, and the **pattern repeat** (0 for plain paper). The number of strips, strips per roll and rolls needed are calculated. 10 cm per strip is allowed for trimming.

## Example

14 m of wall, 2.5 m high, standard 53 cm × 10 m rolls, no repeat:

- Drops = 14 ÷ 0.53 → **27**
- Drops per roll = 10 ÷ 2.6 → **3**
- Rolls = **9**

Buy all rolls from the same batch number so the colour matches.

## Rolls needed by room

Standard 0.53 × 10 m rolls, 2.5 m walls, no pattern repeat:

| Room perimeter | Drops | Rolls |
|---|---|---|
| 10 m | 19 | 7 |
| 12 m | 23 | 8 |
| 14 m | 27 | 9 |
| 16 m | 31 | 11 |
| 18 m | 34 | 12 |
| 20 m | 38 | 13 |

## Pattern repeat

A pattern repeat means each drop must be cut longer so the pattern lines up. Large repeats (over 50 cm) can waste a lot of paper — sometimes a whole drop per roll.

| Match type | What it means |
|---|---|
| Free match | No pattern alignment, least waste |
| Straight match | Pattern aligns horizontally across drops |
| Offset (drop) match | Pattern shifts by half a repeat on alternate drops |

## Roll sizes

| Type | Typical size |
|---|---|
| Standard European roll | 0.53 × 10.05 m |
| US double roll | about 0.53 × 10 m (sold as two single rolls) |
| Wide roll | 0.70 × 10 m |

## Windows and doors

Most decorators do **not** subtract windows and doors from small rooms, because partial drops above and below them still use up paper. For large openings, subtract one drop per full-width door.

## Batch numbers

Buy all rolls with the same batch number, as colours can vary between print runs. Buy one extra roll for repairs.

## Worked example with the default values

The defaults are **14 m of wall**, **2.5 m high**, standard **53 cm × 10 m** rolls and no pattern repeat:

- Drops = 14 ÷ 0.53 = 26.42, rounded up to **27**
- Each drop is cut 2.5 + 0.1 = 2.6 m long, so a roll gives 10 ÷ 2.6 = 3.85, rounded down to **3 drops**
- Rolls = 27 ÷ 3 = 9.0, rounded up to **9 rolls**

For a room, the wall length is the perimeter: a 4.2 m × 3.4 m bedroom has 2 × (4.2 + 3.4) = 15.2 m of wall. A single 3.6 m feature wall at 2.5 m needs 7 drops and **3 rolls**.

## How the pattern repeat changes the order

Take a 2.7 m wall with a 64 cm repeat. Each drop is cut 2.7 + 0.64 + 0.1 = 3.44 m, so a 10 m roll gives only **2 drops** and 14 m of wall needs **14 rolls**, against 9 rolls for plain paper. The calculator adds one full repeat to every drop, which is a safe allowance; a careful hanger who matches each new drop to the last can sometimes save a roll on a big job.

At 2.5 m wall height, 14 m of wall:

| Pattern repeat | Cut length per drop | Drops per roll | Rolls |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 cm | 2.60 m | 3 | 9 |
| 53 cm | 3.13 m | 3 | 9 |
| 64 cm | 3.24 m | 3 | 9 |
| 75 cm | 3.35 m | 2 | 14 |

## Rolls needed by perimeter and wall height

Plain paper, 53 cm × 10 m rolls:

| Wall length | 2.4 m | 2.5 m | 2.7 m | 3.0 m |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 m | 4 | 6 | 6 | 6 |
| 12 m | 6 | 8 | 8 | 8 |
| 16 m | 8 | 11 | 11 | 11 |
| 20 m | 10 | 13 | 13 | 13 |

The big step is between 2.4 m and 2.5 m. A 2.4 m wall is cut at exactly 2.5 m, so a 10 m roll gives 4 drops with nothing to spare, and one miscut costs a drop. From 2.5 m up to about 3.2 m a roll gives 3 drops, and above that only 2, so the number of rolls climbs in steps rather than smoothly with height.

## Measuring a room for wallpaper

Measure each wall along the skirting with a tape, and the height from the top of the skirting to the ceiling or coving at two or three points, using the largest. Walls in older houses are rarely the same height all round. Measure chimney breasts and alcoves along every face, because each return needs its own drops. Read the roll width, length and repeat from the label: the repeat is printed as a figure in cm next to a match symbol.

## Common mistakes

- **Dividing wall area by roll area.** That ignores the offcut left on every roll and usually under-orders by one or two rolls.
- **Using the room width instead of the perimeter.** Enter the total length of every wall to be papered, including returns into window reveals.
- **Forgetting the repeat on an offset (half drop) match.** Use the full repeat printed on the label, not half of it.
- **Ordering exact numbers from different batches.** A later roll from another print run can differ in shade; order a spare from the same batch.
- **Leaving no trim allowance on uneven ceilings.** The 10 cm in the calculator covers normal trimming; add more height if the ceiling line slopes.
