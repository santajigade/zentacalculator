## How to use the pace to speed converter

Type into **any** field: pace per km, pace per mile, km/h or mph. The other three are worked out from it. Pace is entered in decimal minutes, so 6.5 means 6:30.

## Worked example

The default pace is 6 minutes per kilometre:

- Speed = 60 ÷ 6 = **10 km/h**
- Pace per mile = 6 × 1.609344 = 9.656 minutes, which is **9:39 per mile**
- Speed in mph = 60 ÷ 9.656 = **6.21 mph**

So a treadmill set to 10 km/h, or about 6.2 mph, holds a 6:00 per km pace. At that pace a 10 km run takes 60 minutes and a half marathon about 2 hours 7 minutes.

## Treadmills

Treadmills display speed (km/h or mph), while running plans usually use pace. Convert with:

<p class="formula">Pace (min/km) = 60 ÷ speed (km/h)</p>

Setting the treadmill incline to 1% roughly matches the effort of running outdoors on flat ground.

## Pace per km to pace per mile

Multiply min/km by 1.609. 5:00 per km is 8:03 per mile.

## Decimal minutes

The calculator uses decimal minutes: 5.5 min/km means 5:30 per km, not 5:50. To convert, divide the seconds by 60, so 5:45 is 5.75 and 4:20 is 4.333. Typing 5.3 for a 5:30 pace gives 11.32 km/h instead of 10.91 km/h, which is 3.8% too fast on the treadmill.

## Practical notes

- **Treadmill calibration.** Belt speed can be a few percent off the display, especially on older machines, so treat the converted pace as a guide and check effort or heart rate.
- **GPS pace outdoors.** Watch pace jumps around under trees and between buildings; average pace over a whole lap is more reliable than the instant reading.
- **Incline.** A 1% incline is often used to make treadmill running feel closer to flat outdoor running, mainly at faster paces. It does not change the speed conversion.

## Common mistakes

- **Reading decimal minutes as seconds.** 6.5 min/km is 6:30, not 6:50.
- **Inverting the conversion.** Speed is 60 divided by pace, not pace divided by 60; a smaller pace number means a faster speed.
- **Using 1.6 for a mile.** A mile is 1.609344 km. Using 1.6 makes a 6:00 per km pace 9:36 per mile instead of 9:39.
- **Mixing km/h and mph on the treadmill.** Setting 10 on an mph machine is a 6:00 per mile sprint, not a 6:00 per km jog.
- **Converting average speed from split speeds.** Average the times, not the speeds: 1 km at 10 km/h plus 1 km at 15 km/h averages 12 km/h, not 12.5.
