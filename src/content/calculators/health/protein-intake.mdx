## How to use this calculator

Enter your **body weight** and choose the option that best matches your **activity or goal**. You get a daily protein target and a per-meal amount for four meals.

## The formula

<p class="formula">Protein (g/day) = body weight (kg) × g/kg factor</p>

## Worked example

A 70 kg person doing regular strength training (1.6 g/kg):

- Daily protein = 70 × 1.6 = **112 g**
- About **28 g** per meal over four meals
