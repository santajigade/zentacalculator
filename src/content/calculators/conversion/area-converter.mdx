## Converting room dimensions

It is often easier to convert **lengths** first and then multiply. A room 4.2 m × 3.6 m:

- In metres: 15.12 m²
- In feet: 13.78 ft × 11.81 ft = 162.7 ft²

Both give the same area, because 15.12 × 10.764 = 162.7.

## Common mistakes

- **Multiplying by 3.28** instead of 10.764. That converts length, not area.
- **Mixing units**, such as feet for length and metres for width.
- **Forgetting walls and columns** when comparing carpet area with built-up area in property listings.

## Why area conversions square the factor

1 m = 3.28 ft, but 1 m² = 3.28 × 3.28 = **10.76 ft²**. Always convert areas with area units — multiplying square metres by 3.28 is a very common mistake.

## Land units around the world

- **Acre** — USA, UK and many Commonwealth countries.
- **Hectare** — metric countries and international agriculture.
- **Local units** such as the bigha, guntha, cent or marla vary by region — check the local definition before converting.
