## How to use this calculator

Enter **height** and **weight** in any units. Body surface area is shown in m² (or switch units).

## The formula (Mosteller)

<p class="formula">BSA (m²) = √(height (cm) × weight (kg) ÷ 3600)</p>

## Worked example

Height 175 cm, weight 70 kg:

BSA = √(175 × 70 ÷ 3600) = √3.403 = **1.84 m²**
