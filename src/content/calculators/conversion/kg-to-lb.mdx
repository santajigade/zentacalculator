## Definition

Since 1959 the international pound has been defined as **exactly 0.45359237 kg**.

## Mental shortcut

To go from kg to lb, **double it and add 10%**: 70 kg → 140 + 14 = 154 lb. To go from lb to kg, halve it and subtract 10%: 150 lb → 75 − 7.5 = 67.5 kg (exact: 68.04).
