## How to use this calculator

Choose an **activity**, then enter your **body weight** and **duration**. The estimated calories burned appear instantly. To find how long you need to burn a target amount, press × on Duration and type the calories.

## The formula

<p class="formula">kcal = MET × 3.5 × weight (kg) ÷ 200 × minutes</p>

## Worked examples (70 kg, 30 minutes)

| Activity | MET | Calories |
|---|---|---|
| Walking, moderate | 3.5 | 129 |
| Cycling, moderate | 8.0 | 294 |
| Running, 10 km/h | 9.8 | 360 |

## Notes

- A heavier person burns more for the same activity.
- The figure includes your resting calories for that time. Subtract about 1 MET's worth for "extra" calories from the exercise.
- Fitness trackers use similar estimates combined with heart rate.
