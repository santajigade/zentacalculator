    Percentages and everyday maths

    The most used maths tools are the simplest. The percentage calculator answers "what is X% of Y", "X is what percent of Y" and the reverse. For price rises, salary changes or test scores, use percentage change, and for lab work percent error. Other everyday helpers include ratios, fractions, fraction to decimal, averages, weighted averages for grades, and scientific notation and significant figures for science homework.

    Geometry: area, volume and triangles

    Area tools cover the circle, rectangle, triangle, trapezoid, ellipse and regular polygons. For volume there are the cylinder, sphere, cone, pyramid, frustum, capsule and torus. Triangle problems are solved with the Pythagorean theorem, right triangle trigonometry, the law of sines, the law of cosines and the SSS triangle calculator.

    Algebra and higher maths

    Solve a quadratic equation with real or complex roots, a cubic equation, or a 2 by 2 and 3 by 3 system of linear equations. The matrix determinant and inverse calculator handles 3 by 3 matrices step by step. For sequences and growth there are arithmetic and geometric sequences, exponents and logarithms. Engineers will also use linear interpolation to read between table values, plus vectors and complex numbers.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

    Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. The percentage calculator also finds what percent one number is of another.

    Do the calculators show steps?

    Yes. Press “Show steps” to see the formula and working.

    Do the geometry calculators accept different units?

    Yes. You can switch between metric and imperial units, and the area or volume is shown in matching square or cubic units.

    Can the equation solvers give complex roots?

    Yes. The quadratic and cubic solvers show complex roots in a + bi form when the discriminant is negative.
