## How to use this calculator

Fill in **any three** of principal, rate, time and interest (or total amount). The rest is calculated. For example, to find the rate you need, press × on the rate and enter the principal, time and interest you want to earn.

## The formula

<p class="formula">I = P × r × t   A = P + I</p>

- **P** — principal (amount borrowed or invested)
- **r** — annual interest rate as a decimal (6% = 0.06)
- **t** — time in years
- **A** — total amount at the end

## Worked example

50,000 invested at 6% simple interest for 3 years:

- I = 50,000 × 0.06 × 3 = **9,000**
- A = 50,000 + 9,000 = **59,000**

## Simple vs compound interest

For the same 50,000 at 6%:

| Years | Simple interest total | Compound (yearly) total |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 53,000 | 53,000 |
| 3 | 59,000 | 59,550.80 |
| 10 | 80,000 | 89,542.38 |
| 20 | 110,000 | 160,356.77 |

The gap widens over time. See the [compound interest calculator](/finance/compound-interest/) for growth with compounding.

## Where simple interest is used

- Some short-term personal and car loans
- Certain bonds and treasury bills
- Late-payment interest on invoices
- Quick estimates before using a more detailed model
