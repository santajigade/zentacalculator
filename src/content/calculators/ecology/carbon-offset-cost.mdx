## How to use the carbon offset cost calculator

Enter the **emissions** to offset (yearly) and the **price per tonne**. The total and monthly cost are calculated.

## Worked example with the default values

You want to offset 5 tonnes of CO2 a year and the provider charges 15 per tonne:

1. Yearly cost = 5 t × 15 per tonne = **75.00**
2. Monthly cost = 75.00 ÷ 12 = **6.25**

The calculator stores mass in kilograms, so 5 t is treated as 5,000 kg and divided by 1,000 before it is multiplied by the price. The currency is whatever you type: the price and the result are always in the same unit.

## Cost of offsetting 5 tonnes at different prices

| Price per tonne | Per year | Per month | Per day |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 25.00 | 2.08 | 0.07 |
| 15 | 75.00 | 6.25 | 0.21 |
| 30 | 150.00 | 12.50 | 0.41 |
| 50 | 250.00 | 20.83 | 0.68 |
| 100 | 500.00 | 41.67 | 1.37 |
| 600 | 3,000.00 | 250.00 | 8.22 |

The spread is the main point: the same 5 tonnes can cost less than a cinema ticket a month with cheap avoidance credits or several hundred a month with engineered removals such as direct air capture.

## Typical offset prices

| Offset type | Typical price per tonne CO₂ (voluntary market) |
|---|---|
| Renewable energy credits | low, often under 5 |
| Cookstoves and community projects | about 5–15 |
| Forestry and nature-based | about 5–30 |
| Engineered removals (e.g. direct air capture) | often 300–1,000 |

Prices vary widely by project, standard and year.

## What makes a good offset

- **Additional**: the reduction would not have happened anyway.
- **Permanent**: the carbon stays out of the atmosphere (a risk for forests that may burn).
- **Verified**: certified by a recognised standard.
- **No double counting**: credits are retired in a public registry.

## Reduce first, then offset

Offsets are most credible as a complement to cutting emissions, not a replacement. Measure your footprint, cut what you can with the [car](/ecology/car-carbon-footprint/), [flight](/ecology/flight-emissions/) and [electricity](/ecology/electricity-co2/) calculators, then offset the rest.

## Average footprints

| Region | Approximate CO₂ per person per year |
|---|---|
| Global average | 4–5 t |
| European Union | 6–7 t |
| United States | 14–15 t |
| India | about 2 t |

Figures are territorial emissions from fossil fuels and industry; consumption-based footprints can differ.

## Offsetting a typical footprint

Using the per person averages from the table above, here is what a full year of emissions would cost to offset at three price levels:

| Footprint | At 15 per t | At 30 per t | At 100 per t |
|---|---|---|---|
| India average (about 2 t) | 30.00 | 60.00 | 200.00 |
| Global average (about 4.7 t) | 70.50 | 141.00 | 470.00 |
| EU average (about 6 t) | 90.00 | 180.00 | 600.00 |
| US average (about 14.5 t) | 217.50 | 435.00 | 1,450.00 |

A single return long haul flight of about 2 t costs 30.00 to offset at 15 per tonne. Work out the tonnes first with the [flight emissions calculator](/ecology/flight-emissions/), then bring the figure here.

## Practical notes

- **Check what one credit covers.** One credit is normally one tonne of CO2 equivalent. If a site sells offsets "per flight" or "per month", divide its price by the tonnes it claims to cover to compare it with other providers.
- **Expect fees.** Retail providers often add a service margin. A 10% fee turns the 75.00 example into 82.50.
- **Small amounts.** For 800 kg enter 0.8 t; at 15 per tonne that is 12.00.
- **Reduce, then offset the remainder.** Cutting the footprint in half before buying credits halves the bill, so 2.5 t at 15 costs 37.50 instead of 75.00.
- **Ask for retirement proof.** A good provider retires the credits on a public registry and gives you the serial numbers.

## Common mistakes

- **Mixing kilograms and tonnes.** Footprint tools often report kilograms; 5,000 kg is 5 t, not 5,000 t.
- **Comparing prices of different credit types.** Avoidance credits and removal credits are different products, so a lower price per tonne does not mean a better deal.
- **Offsetting CO2 only when the figure is CO2e.** Some footprints include methane and other gases as CO2 equivalent; buy credits for the CO2e total.
- **Forgetting it is a yearly figure.** The monthly value is the yearly cost divided by 12, so enter a full year of emissions to get a correct monthly budget.
- **Buying unverified credits.** Credits without an independent standard and registry can be double counted or never delivered.
