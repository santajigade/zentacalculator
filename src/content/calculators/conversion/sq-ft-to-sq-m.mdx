## How to use the square feet to square meters converter

Type the area in **square feet** or **square metres** and the other updates instantly.

## Square feet to square meters formula

<p class="formula">m² = ft² × 0.092903   ft² = m² × 10.7639</p>

**Example:** a 1,000 sq ft flat is 1,000 × 0.092903 = **92.9 m²**. A 50 m² office is **538 sq ft**.

## Carpet, built-up and super built-up area

Property listings may quote different areas. **Carpet area** is the usable floor inside the walls. **Built-up area** adds wall thickness and balconies, and **super built-up area** adds a share of common areas. Always check which one a price is based on before comparing.

## Price per square foot

To compare properties, divide the price by the area. A ₹75,00,000 flat of 1,000 sq ft costs ₹7,500 per sq ft, or about ₹80,730 per m².

## Calculating floor area

Multiply length × width for each rectangular room and add them up. The [rectangle calculator](/math/rectangle/) handles mixed units.
