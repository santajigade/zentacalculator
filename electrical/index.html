    For LV and MV power systems

    The calculators cover single-phase, three-phase and DC circuits at common Indian, European and North American voltages (230/415 V, 400 V, 120/208/480 V and 11 kV). Formulas follow IEC 60364, IEC 60076, IEEE 519 and NEMA MG 1 where relevant, and each page explains the assumptions.

    From quick checks to design

    Use them for quick site checks — will this cable drop too much voltage, how many amps will this motor draw — or as a starting point for design. For final designs, always confirm against manufacturer data and your local wiring regulations.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I convert kW to amps for a three-phase motor?

    I = P ÷ (√3 × V × PF × efficiency). Use the kW to amps or three-phase motor power calculator, which apply this formula for you.

    Do these calculators work for 50 Hz and 60 Hz systems?

    Yes. Where frequency matters — such as power factor correction or motor speed — you can enter 50 or 60 Hz.

    Are the results suitable for final design?

    They are accurate for the formulas shown, but final designs must also consider installation conditions, derating and local regulations.
