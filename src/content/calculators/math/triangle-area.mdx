## How to use this calculator

Enter the **base** and **height** to get the area. You can also enter the area and one dimension to find the other — useful when a plan gives you the area and the base.

## The formula

<p class="formula">A = ½ × b × h</p>

The **height** is the perpendicular distance from the base to the opposite corner — not the length of a sloping side.

## Why one half?

Any triangle is exactly half of a parallelogram with the same base and height. A parallelogram's area is base × height, so the triangle gets half of that.

## Worked example

A triangular garden bed has a 4 m base and is 2.5 m deep (measured straight out from the base).

A = ½ × 4 × 2.5 = **5 m²**

At 40 litres of compost per m², you would need 200 litres.

## Which side is the base?

Any side can be the base, as long as you use the height measured to that side. All three choices give the same area.

## Right triangles

In a right triangle, the two sides that meet at the right angle are a base and its height. A 3-4-5 triangle has an area of ½ × 3 × 4 = 6. Use the [Pythagorean theorem calculator](/math/pythagorean-theorem/) to find a missing side first.
