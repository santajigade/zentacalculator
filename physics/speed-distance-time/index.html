    Speed is distance divided by time. This simple relationship answers everyday questions — how long a journey takes, how fast you need to go or how far you will travel. This free speed, distance & time calculator solves for whichever value you leave blank and shows the formula and steps.

    How to use the speed, distance & time calculator

    Enter any two of distance, speed and time — the third is calculated. Each field has its own unit, so you can mix kilometres with mph or minutes with hours.

    Speed, distance & time formula

    distance = speed × time speed = distance ÷ time time = distance ÷ speed

    Worked examples

    • Travel time: 150 km at 60 km/h → 150 ÷ 60 = 2.5 h (2 h 30 min).
    • Average speed: 42.195 km marathon in 3 h 30 min → 42.195 ÷ 3.5 = 12.06 km/h.
    • Distance: walking at 5 km/h for 45 minutes → 5 × 0.75 = 3.75 km.

    Converting decimal hours

    0.5 h = 30 min · 0.25 h = 15 min · 0.1 h = 6 min. Switch the Time unit to minutes to avoid conversions.

    Average speed over several legs

    Average speed is total distance ÷ total time, not the average of the speeds. Driving 60 km at 60 km/h (1 h) and 60 km at 120 km/h (0.5 h) gives 120 km in 1.5 h — an average of 80 km/h, not 90.

    Relative speed

    • Moving in the same direction: subtract speeds. A car at 100 km/h overtaking a truck at 80 km/h closes at 20 km/h.
    • Moving towards each other: add speeds. Two trains 300 km apart at 80 and 70 km/h meet after 300 ÷ 150 = 2 hours.

    Pace

    Runners and walkers often use pace (minutes per km), which is the inverse of speed. 12 km/h = 5 min/km. See the pace calculator.

    Keep units consistent

    The most common mistake is mixing units by hand, such as metres with km/h. The calculator converts every field automatically — just pick the unit you have.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the formula for speed?

    Speed = distance ÷ time. Distance = speed × time, and time = distance ÷ speed.

    How long does 150 km take at 60 km/h?

    150 ÷ 60 = 2.5 hours, which is 2 hours 30 minutes.

    What is average speed?

    Total distance divided by total time, including stops. It is usually lower than your cruising speed.

    How do I find speed from distance and time?

    Speed = distance ÷ time. 120 km in 1.5 hours is 80 km/h.
