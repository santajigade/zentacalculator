## How to use the miles to KM converter

Type a value in **Miles** and the other units update instantly. Every field is live, so you can just as easily start from kilometres or any other unit and convert back.

## Miles to KM formula

<p class="formula">km = miles × 1.609344</p>

1 mi = 1.60934 km, and 1 km = 0.621371 mi.

## Definitions

- **Mile (statute)**: exactly 1,609.344 m, or 5,280 ft.
- **Kilometre**: 1,000 m, about 0.6214 miles.
- **Nautical mile**: 1,852 m — used at sea and in aviation, not on roads.

## The Fibonacci trick

Consecutive Fibonacci numbers (3, 5, 8, 13, 21, 34…) are close to the miles-to-km ratio: 5 miles ≈ 8 km, 8 miles ≈ 13 km, 13 miles ≈ 21 km.

## Driving

Distances on UK and US road signs are in miles; most other countries use km. A 300-mile road trip is about 483 km.
